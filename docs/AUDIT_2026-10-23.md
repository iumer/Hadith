# Heart Threads — full audit (UI · functionality · content)

**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Branch:** `cursor/app-interactive-mockup-db55` @ `0d8c101`
**How it was tested:**
- **The app:** run through Expo Go on the iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 27), the same path as `docs/INSTALL.md`. I walked Name → Feeling → Subs → Threads → Read → Report → Back×3.
- **The content:** all 182 passages in `app/src/content/catalog.json` checked by script against the built `data/build/hadith.db` and `data/build/quran.db`, then read by hand for fit.
- **Typecheck:** `tsc --noEmit` passes.
- **Not covered:** Android was not run (no emulator here). Gesture feel on a real phone still needs your device check.

**Nothing in the app was changed.** This file is suggestions only.

> ⚠️ **Religious content note:** the suggested replacements were chosen from authentic (sahih/hasan) narrations, with each grade checked against the index. The Meaning and Reflection texts were written in the app's existing voice. Please have a qualified scholar review them before shipping.

## 4. Content: problems across the whole catalog

These need a **catalog rebuild**, not hand edits. The `.txt` source files are mostly fine.

1. **English cut off at the first `"`** on 33 Quran cards: 9:59, 10:58 (×2), 89:27 (×2), 2:249, 3:173 (×2), 41:30, 3:154 (×2), 2:156, 12:84, 12:86 (×2), 9:40 (×2), 20:46 (×3), 2:286 (×3), 15:56, 39:53 (×3), 21:103, 9:51, 20:68, 26:62, 66:8, 3:26.
2. **2:286 shows the full Arabic but only half the English.** The du'a "Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us…" is missing, even though it's the most comforting part for Burned Out / Drowning / Helpless.
3. **Hadith "matn" extraction picked the wrong quote** (the Arabic and English come from different sentences): Bukhari 1283, 1303, 2908; Muslim 923a, 2307a; Tirmidhi 2517.
4. **Leftover narrator/chain text in the English:** Ibn Majah 3818, Nasa'i 5453 (no content at all), Muslim 2675a ("that Allah, the Exalted…"), Muslim 2610a, Abu Dawud 4777, 4782, 3330, and Ibn Majah 4251 (stray `’.`). Nawawi 19's Arabic includes the isnad.
5. **The broken-Arabic-word bug**: a stray space before ا after tanween on about 40 cards. Fix: `re.sub(r'([ࣰ-ًࣲ-ٍ]) ا', r'\1ا', text)` at build time.
6. **Authenticity:** Abu Dawud 1518 is **da'if** (×3). Ibn Majah 4250, the usual alternative ("the one who repents is like one who has no sin"), is **also da'if** in this index, so it was deliberately *not* suggested. Everything else in the app is sahih/hasan.
7. **Duplicates:** 182 cards contain only 132 unique passages. The worst are same-hadith repeats *inside one list*: Content (Bukhari 6446 = Muslim 1051); Assured and Loneliness (Bukhari 7405 = Muslim 2675a); Fear and Insecurity (Tirmidhi 2516 = Nawawi 19); Anger (Bukhari 6114 = Muslim 2609a; Muslim 2610a ≈ Tirmidhi 3452); Feeling Wronged (Bukhari 2447 ≈ Muslim 2578); Resentment (Bukhari 6065 = Muslim 2558b = Tirmidhi 1935); Guilt and Regret (Tirmidhi 2499 = Ibn Majah 4251); Doubt (Bukhari 52 = Abu Dawud 3330); Worry (Bukhari 6369 = Nasa'i 5453).
8. **Thin lists:** Emptiness (2), Defeated / Hopeless / Humiliation / Conflicted / Excited / Joyful / Dread (3 each). Several have no hadith at all.

## 5. Content: passage-by-passage changes

Format: **Mood > Sub — `current reference` → action**, then the **current card exactly as the app shows it today**, then the **suggested card** in the same layout as the app (Arabic · English · Meaning · Reflection).

Actions:
- `REPLACE`: swap the passage for a better one.
- `FIX TEXT`: same passage, but the app shows the wrong or cut-off text.
- `MERGE`: join fragmented ayahs into one card.
- `ADD`: list is too thin.
- `FIX REFERENCE` / `FIX LABEL`: no text change needed.

## Happy

### 1. Happy > Content — `Sahih Muslim 1051` → **REPLACE**

**Why:** Same hadith as Sahih al-Bukhari 6446, which is already the card right before it in this list ("richness is richness of the soul"). The user reads the same words twice.

**CURRENT — Sahih Muslim 1051**  
<sub>As it appears in the app today</sub>

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar" style="font-size:1.3em;line-height:2">لَيْسَ الْغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ الْعَرَضِ وَلَكِنَّ الْغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ</p>

> **English:** Richness does not lie in the abundance of (worldly) goods but richness is the richness of the soul (heart, self).
>
> **Meaning:** The Prophet ﷺ teaches that richness does not lie in abundant worldly goods, but in richness of the soul.<br>Outer plenty can coexist with inner poverty. Inner contentment can make modest means feel complete.<br>This hadith redirects our measure of wealth from what we hold to how our heart rests.
>
> **Reflection:** Pray for a heart that feels enough.<br>True richness begins where comparison ends.

**SUGGESTED — Sahih Muslim 2963a**  
<sub>Grade: Sahih (Sahih Muslim)</sub>

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar" style="font-size:1.3em;line-height:2">إِذَا نَظَرَ أَحَدُكُمْ إِلَى مَنْ فُضِّلَ عَلَيْهِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْخَلْقِ فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْهُ مِمَّنْ فُضِّلَ عَلَيْهِ</p>

> **English:** When one of you looks at someone who has been given more than him in wealth and appearance, let him also look at someone who has been given less than him.
>
> **Meaning:** The Prophet ﷺ gives a simple habit for a content heart: when you notice someone who has more, also notice someone who has less.<br>Comparison only upward breeds restlessness; looking at the whole picture brings gratitude.<br>Contentment is protected by where we let our eyes rest.
>
> **Reflection:** Look at what you have been given before you look at what others have.<br>The blessing you overlook today is someone else's prayer.

### 3. Happy > Excited — `Quran 61:13` → **REPLACE**

**Why:** 61:13 is the promise of military victory and conquest in a battle context. It doesn't speak to everyday excitement and needs tafsir to read correctly.

**CURRENT — Quran 61:13**  
<sub>As it appears in the app today</sub>

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar" style="font-size:1.3em;line-height:2">وَأُخۡرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَاۖ نَصۡرࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَتۡحࣱ قَرِيبࣱۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ</p>

> **English:** And [you will obtain] another [favor] that you love - victory from Allah and an imminent conquest; and give good tidings to the believers
>
> **Meaning:** Allah tells us that the believers of another favor they love: victory from Allah and an imminent conquest—and to give good tidings to the believers.<br>Hope is part of the message; glad news is meant to lift hearts that strive.<br>Allah announces openings before they fully appear.
>
> **Reflection:** Hold the good tidings Allah gives believers.<br>Victory from Him can be nearer than your worry suggests.

**SUGGESTED — Sunan Abi Dawud 2774**  
<sub>Grade: Sahih (Al-Albani)</sub>

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar" style="font-size:1.3em;line-height:2">كَانَ إِذَا جَاءَهُ أَمْرُ سُرُورٍ أَوْ بُشِّرَ بِهِ خَرَّ سَاجِدًا شَاكِرًا لِلَّهِ</p>

> **English:** When something came to the Prophet ﷺ that made him happy, or he was given good news, he would fall into prostration in gratitude to Allah.
>
> **Meaning:** The Prophet ﷺ met moments of excitement by turning straight to Allah in sujud.<br>Joy is not meant to pull us away from Allah; it is a reason to come closer.<br>This is the sunnah 'sajdat ash-shukr' — a prostration of thanks.
>
> **Reflection:** When good news reaches you, let your first response be gratitude.<br>Put your forehead down for the One who sent it.
