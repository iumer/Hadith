Quran 3:135-136

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا فَعَلُواْ فَٰحِشَةً أَوۡ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ ذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرُواْ لِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَمَن يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَمۡ يُصِرُّواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلُواْ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ ﴿١٣٥﴾ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ جَزَآؤُهُم مَّغۡفِرَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَجَنَّٰتࣱ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَنِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ ﴿١٣٦﴾

Urdu Translation:

(135) جب ان سے کوئی ناشائستہ کام ہو جائے یا کوئی گناه کر بیٹھیں تو فوراً اللہ کا ذکر اور اپنے گناہوں کے لئے استغفار کرتے ہیں، فیالواقع اللہ تعالیٰ کے سوا اور کون گناہوں کو بخش سکتا ہے؟ اور وه لوگ باوجود علم کے کسی برے کام پر اڑ نہیں جاتے (136) انہیں کا بدلہ ان کے رب کی طرف سے مغفرت ہے اور جنتیں ہیں جن کے نیچے نہریں بہتی ہیں، جن میں وه ہمیشہ رہیں گے، ان نیک کاموں کے کرنے والوں کا ﺛواب کیا ہی اچھا ہے

English Translation:

“(135) And those who, when they commit an immorality or wrong themselves [by transgression], remember Allah and seek forgiveness for their sins - and who can forgive sins except Allah? - and [who] do not persist in what they have done while they know (136) Those - their reward is forgiveness from their Lord and gardens beneath which rivers flow [in Paradise], wherein they will abide eternally; and excellent is the reward of the [righteous] workers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah praises those who, when they commit a wrong or wrong themselves, remember Allah and seek forgiveness — and who forgives sins except Allah? — and do not persist knowingly. Their reward is forgiveness from their Lord and Gardens beneath which rivers flow.

Reflection

Let regret move you, not crush you.

Remember Him, ask forgiveness, and don't go back.

Reference: Quran 3:135-136, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 4:17

Surah An-Nisaa (سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ)

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا ٱلتَّوۡبَةُ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ لِلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلسُّوٓءَ بِجَهَٰلَةࣲ ثُمَّ يَتُوبُونَ مِن قَرِيبࣲ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يَتُوبُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ صرف انہی لوگوں کی توبہ قبول فرماتا ہے جو بوجہ نادانی کوئی برائی کر گزریں پھر جلد اس سے باز آ جائیں اور توبہ کریں تو اللہ تعالیٰ بھی ان کی توبہ قبول کرتا ہے، اللہ تعالیٰ بڑے علم واﻻ حکمت واﻻ ہے

English Translation:

“The repentance accepted by Allah is only for those who do wrong in ignorance [or carelessness] and then repent soon after. It is those to whom Allah will turn in forgiveness, and Allah is ever Knowing and Wise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that accepted repentance is for those who do wrong in ignorance and then repent soon after—those to whom Allah turns in forgiveness, and He is Knowing and Wise.

Haste in returning matters. Delaying softens the resolve to change.

Allah’s turning toward the repentant is full of knowledge and wisdom.

Reflection

When you slip, return quickly.

Swift repentance meets a Lord who turns to you with forgiveness.

Reference: Quran 4:17, Surah An-Nisaa.

Quran 39:53

Surah Az-Zumar (سُورَةُ الزُّمَرِ)

Arabic:

۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Urdu Translation:

(میری جانب سے) کہہ دو کہ اے میرے بندو! جنہوں نے اپنی جانوں پر زیادتی کی ہے تم اللہ کی رحمت سے ناامید نہ ہو جاؤ، بالیقین اللہ تعالیٰ سارے گناہوں کو بخش دیتا ہے، واقعی وه بڑی بخشش بڑی رحمت واﻻ ہے

English Translation:

“Say, "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves [by sinning], do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to say to His servants who have wronged themselves: do not despair of the mercy of Allah—indeed, Allah forgives all sins, for He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

No pile of mistakes is thicker than His mercy for those who turn back.

Despair is refused; forgiveness is offered widely.

Reflection

Do not despair of Allah’s mercy, no matter what you have done.

He forgives sins completely—He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

Reference: Quran 39:53, Surah Az-Zumar.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3537

Book 48: Chapters on Supplication (كتاب الدعوات عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Ibn `Umar narrated that the Prophet (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَقْبَلُ تَوْبَةَ الْعَبْدِ مَا لَمْ يُغَرْغِرْ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ اپنے بندے کی توبہ اس وقت تک قبول کرتا ہے جب تک کہ ( موت کے وقت ) اس کے گلے سے خر خر کی آواز نہ آنے لگے

English Translation:

“Indeed Allah accepts the repentance of a slave as long as (his soul does not reach his throat).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that Allah accepts a servant’s repentance as long as the soul has not reached the throat.

The door stays open through life’s length—until the final throes of death.

Urgency remains, but so does wide hope while breath remains.

Reflection

Repent while life remains—Allah still accepts you.

Do not wait until the door of return is closing.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3537, Book of Chapters on Supplication.

Sahih Muslim 2749

Book 50: The Book of Repentance (كتاب التوبة)

Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) having said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَوْ لَمْ تُذْنِبُوا لَذَهَبَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ وَلَجَاءَ بِقَوْمٍ يُذْنِبُونَ فَيَسْتَغْفِرُونَ اللَّهَ فَيَغْفِرُ لَهُمْ

Urdu Translation:

اس ذات کی قسم جس کے ہاتھ میں میری جان ہے! اگر تم ( لوگ ) گناہ نہ کرو تو اللہ تعالیٰ تم کو ( اس دنیا سے ) لے جائے اور ( تمہارے بدلے میں ) ایسی قوم کو لے آئے جو گناہ کریں اور اللہ تعالیٰ سے مغفرت مانگیں تو وہ ان کی مغفرت فرمائے

English Translation:

“By Him in Whose Hand is my life, if you were not to commit sin, Allah would sweep you out of existence and He would replace (you by) those people who would commit sin and seek forgiveness from Allah, and He would have pardoned them.”

Meaning ❤️

If you did not sin, Allah would replace you with people who sin and then seek His forgiveness, and He would forgive them.

This is not permission to sin; it shows that Allah loves those who come back.

Reflection

You were never expected to be perfect.

You were invited to come back. Come back.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2749, Book of The Book of Repentance.
