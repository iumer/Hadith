Quran 4:110

Surah An-Nisaa (سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ)

Arabic:

وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ سُوٓءًا أَوۡ يَظۡلِمۡ نَفۡسَهُۥ ثُمَّ يَسۡتَغۡفِرِ ٱللَّهَ يَجِدِ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣰ ا رَّحِيمࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And whoever does a wrong or wrongs himself but then seeks forgiveness of Allah will find Allah Forgiving and Merciful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that whoever does wrong or wrongs himself, then seeks Allah’s forgiveness, will find Allah Forgiving and Merciful.

The door after sin is seeking forgiveness—and finding Him ready with maghfirah and rahmah.

No self-harm of the soul is beyond repair when we turn back.

Reflection

If you have wronged yourself, seek Allah’s forgiveness now.

You will find Him Forgiving and Merciful.

Reference: Quran 4:110, Surah An-Nisaa.

Quran 39:53

Surah Az-Zumar (سُورَةُ الزُّمَرِ)

Arabic:

۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

English Translation:

“Say, "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves [by sinning], do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to say to His servants who have wronged themselves: do not despair of the mercy of Allah—indeed, Allah forgives all sins, for He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

No pile of mistakes is thicker than His mercy for those who turn back.

Despair is refused; forgiveness is offered widely.

Reflection

Do not despair of Allah’s mercy, no matter what you have done.

He forgives sins completely—He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

Reference: Quran 39:53, Surah Az-Zumar.

Quran 66:8

Surah At-Tahrim (سُورَةُ التَّحۡرِيمِ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوۡبَةࣰ نَّصُوحًا عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يُكَفِّرَ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِكُمۡ وَيُدۡخِلَكُمۡ جَنَّٰتࣲ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ يَوۡمَ لَا يُخۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّبِيَّ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥۖ نُورُهُمۡ يَسۡعَىٰ بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَبِأَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ أَتۡمِمۡ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدِيرࣱ‏

English Translation:

“O you who have believed, repent to Allah with sincere repentance. Perhaps your Lord will remove from you your misdeeds and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow [on] the Day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and those who believed with him. Their light will proceed before them and on their right; they will say, "Our Lord, perfect for us our light and forgive us. Indeed, You are over all things competent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to repent to Him with sincere repentance—hoping your Lord will remove misdeeds and admit you to gardens, on a Day when light proceeds before the believers as they ask Allah to perfect their light and forgive them.

Tawbah nasuh is earnest, reforming return.

Light and forgiveness are the hope of those who repent sincerely.

Reflection

Repent to Allah with a sincerity that changes you.

Ask Him to perfect your light and forgive what remains.

Reference: Quran 66:8, Surah At-Tahrim.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Shaddad bin Aus (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

سَيِّدُ الاِسْتِغْفَارِ أَنْ تَقُولَ اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَىَّ وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لاَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ

English Translation:

“The most superior way of asking for forgiveness from Allah is: 'Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta, Khalaqtani wa ana `Abduka, wa ana `ala `ahdika wa wa`dika mastata`tu, A`udhu bika min Sharri ma sana`tu, abu'u Laka bini`matika `alaiya, wa abu'u laka bidhanbi faghfir lee fa innahu la yaghfiru adhdhunuba illa anta.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches the most superior form of seeking forgiveness—Sayyid al-Istighfar—which affirms Allah as Lord, admits our weakness, and asks for forgiveness of sins that only He can forgive.

He encouraged reciting it with firm faith morning and evening, linking it to a beautiful hope of Paradise if one dies that day or night.

Turning to Allah with recognition and humility is among the best of deeds.

Reflection

Begin and end your day by admitting your need of Allah’s forgiveness.

A sincere istighfar keeps the heart soft and hopeful.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6306, Book of Invocations.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2499

Book 37: Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara' (كتاب صفة القيامة والرقائق والورع عن رسول الله صلى الله)

Anas narrated that the Prophet (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

كُلُّ ابْنِ آدَمَ خَطَّاءٌ وَخَيْرُ الْخَطَّائِينَ التَّوَّابُونَ

English Translation:

“Every son of Adam sins, and the best of the sinners are the repentant.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that every child of Adam sins, and the best of sinners are those who repent.

The excellence is not in never falling, but in returning.

Repentance turns a human pattern of error into a path of hope.

Reflection

You will err—be among the best by repenting.

Returning to Allah is the beauty after the fall.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2499, Book of Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara'.

Sunan Ibn Majah 4251

Book 37: Zuhd (كتاب الزهد)

It was narrated from Anas that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

كُلُّ بَنِي آدَمَ خَطَّاءٌ وَخَيْرُ الْخَطَّائِينَ التَّوَّابُونَ

English Translation:

“Every son of Adam commits sin, and the best of those who commit sin are those who repent.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that every child of Adam sins, and the best of those who sin are those who repent.

The standard is not a flawless past; it is a heart that turns back. Falling is human; remaining distant is what hardens the soul.

Repentance is always within reach for those who seek it.

Reflection

Your worth before Allah is not measured by never failing, but by returning.

Be among those who repent—again and again.

Reference: Sunan Ibn Majah 4251, Book of Zuhd.
