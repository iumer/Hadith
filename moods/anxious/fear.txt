Quran 2:155

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُم بِشَيۡءࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ وَٱلۡجُوعِ وَنَقۡصࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَنفُسِ وَٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

English Translation:

“And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that we will surely be tested with fear, hunger, and loss of wealth, lives, and fruits—yet He gives good news to the patient.

Trials are part of the path, not proof of abandonment.

Glad tidings belong to those who meet testing with sabr.

Reflection

Hardship does not mean Allah has left you—it may be the test He told you would come.

Patience carries a promise of good news.

Reference: Quran 2:155, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 3:175

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ يُخَوِّفُ أَوۡلِيَآءَهُۥ فَلَا تَخَافُوهُمۡ وَخَافُونِ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

English Translation:

“That is only Satan who frightens [you] of his supporters. So fear them not, but fear Me, if you are [indeed] believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that it is only Satan who frightens you of his supporters—so do not fear them, but fear Me, if you are believers.

Fear is sometimes inflated by whispers that magnify people and shrink trust in Allah.

The believer’s awe is redirected toward Allah alone.

Reflection

Do not let Satan inflate your fear of people.

Keep your reverence for Allah, and watch lesser fears lose their grip.

Reference: Quran 3:175, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 8:2

Surah Al-Anfaal (سُورَةُ الأَنفَالِ)

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

English Translation:

“The believers are only those who, when Allah is mentioned, their hearts become fearful, and when His verses are recited to them, it increases them in faith; and upon their Lord they rely”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the believers are those whose hearts tremble when He is mentioned, whose faith increases when His verses are recited, and who rely upon their Lord.

Living faith feels awe, grows with revelation, and rests in tawakkul.

These are signs of a heart awake to Allah.

Reflection

Let Allah’s mention move your heart and His verses increase your faith.

Rely upon your Lord in what you cannot control.

Reference: Quran 8:2, Surah Al-Anfaal.

Quran 10:62

Surah Yunus (سُورَةُ يُونُسَ)

Arabic:

أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ

English Translation:

“Unquestionably, [for] the allies of Allah there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that for His allies there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve.

Those who walk closely with Allah are promised a security that reaches beyond the tremors of this world.

Friendship with Allah steadies the heart against dread of what is coming and sorrow over what has passed.

Reflection

Draw near to Allah and let His friendship quiet your fears.

For those close to Him, grief and fear do not have the final word.

Reference: Quran 10:62, Surah Yunus.

Quran 20:46

Surah Taa-Haa (سُورَةُ طه)

Arabic:

قَالَ لَا تَخَافَآۖ إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَآ أَسۡمَعُ وَأَرَىٰ

English Translation:

“[Allah] said, "Fear not. Indeed, I am with you both; I hear and I see”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He said to Musa and Harun, “Fear not. Indeed, I am with you both; I hear and I see.”

Before a fearful mission, Allah replaces dread with the assurance of His presence, hearing, and sight.

Knowing Allah is with you changes how you face what once terrified you.

Reflection

When fear rises, remember: Allah hears and sees, and He is with you.

His companionship is stronger than what stands in front of you.

Reference: Quran 20:46, Surah Taa-Haa.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2516

Book 37: Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara' (كتاب صفة القيامة والرقائق والورع عن رسول الله صلى الله)

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas narrated (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَا غُلاَمُ إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الأُمَّةَ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلاَّ بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ لَكَ وَلَوِ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ إِلاَّ بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكَ رُفِعَتِ الأَقْلاَمُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُفُ

English Translation:

“'O boy! I will teach you a statement: Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you. Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him before you. When you ask, ask Allah, and when you seek aid, seek Allah's aid. Know that if the entire creation were to gather together to do something to benefit you- you would never get any benefit except that Allah had written for you. And if they were to gather to do something to harm you- you would never be harmed except that Allah had written for you. The pens are lifted and the pages are dried.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches: be mindful of Allah and He will protect you; be mindful of Him and you will find Him before you. Ask Allah and seek His aid. If all creation gathered to benefit or harm you, they could only do what He has written.

The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried.

Mindfulness of Allah brings protection and clarity about decree.

Reflection

Keep Allah before you in your choices, and seek help from Him alone.

What was written will reach you—walk with mindfulness, not fear of creation.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2516, Book of Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara'.

The Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi 19

Book 1: Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi

It is narrated:

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: "كُنْت خَلْفَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم يَوْمًا، فَقَالَ: يَا غُلَامِ! إنِّي أُعَلِّمُك كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظْ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْك، احْفَظْ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَك، إذَا سَأَلْت فَاسْأَلْ اللَّهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْت فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاَللَّهِ، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْأُمَّةَ لَوْ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوك بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوك إلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ لَك، وَإِنْ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوك بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوك إلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْك؛ رُفِعَتْ الْأَقْلَامُ، وَجَفَّتْ الصُّحُفُ" . رَوَاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ [رقم:2516] وَقَالَ: حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ. وَفِي رِوَايَةِ غَيْرِ التِّرْمِذِيِّ: "احْفَظْ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ أمامك، تَعَرَّفْ إلَى اللَّهِ فِي الرَّخَاءِ يَعْرِفُك فِي الشِّدَّةِ، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ مَا أَخْطَأَك لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُصِيبَك، وَمَا أَصَابَك لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُخْطِئَك، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ النَّصْرَ مَعَ الصَّبْرِ، وَأَنْ الْفَرَجَ مَعَ الْكَرْبِ، وَأَنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

English Translation:

“O young man, I shall teach you some words [of advice]: Be mindful of Allah and Allah will protect you. Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him in front of you. If you ask, then ask Allah [alone]; and if you seek help, then seek help from Allah [alone]. And know that if the nation were to gather together to benefit you with anything, they would not benefit you except with what Allah had already prescribed for you. And if they were to gather together to harm you with anything, they would not harm you except with what Allah had already prescribed against you. The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches a young man words of counsel: be mindful of Allah and He will protect you; be mindful of Him and you will find Him before you.

Ask Allah alone and seek help from Him alone. If the whole nation gathered to benefit or harm you, they could only do what Allah has already written.

Pens have been lifted and pages dried—so walk with trust.

Reflection

Keep Allah in mind, and you will find Him near and protecting.

What reaches you was written; what misses you was never meant to stay.

Reference: The Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi 19, Book of Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi.
