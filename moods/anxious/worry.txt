Quran 13:28

Surah Ar-Ra'd (سُورَةُ الرَّعۡدِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

Urdu Translation:

جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے ان کے دل اللہ کے ذکر سے اطمینان حاصل کرتے ہیں۔ یاد رکھو اللہ کے ذکر سے ہی دلوں کو تسلی حاصل ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that hearts find assurance through His remembrance—and that by the remembrance of Allah, hearts are assured.

Dhikr is not empty repetition; it is the means by which restless hearts settle.

Belief and remembrance together bring a calm the world cannot manufacture.

Reflection

When your heart races, remember Allah until it softens.

True assurance grows in His remembrance.

Reference: Quran 13:28, Surah Ar-Ra'd.

Quran 2:286

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرࣰ ا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ کسی جان کو اس کی طاقت سے زیاده تکلیف نہیں دیتا، جو نیکی وه کرے وه اس کے لئے اور جو برائی وه کرے وه اس پر ہے، اے ہمارے رب! اگر ہم بھول گئے ہوں یا خطا کی ہو تو ہمیں نہ پکڑنا، اے ہمارے رب! ہم پر وه بوجھ نہ ڈال جو ہم سے پہلے لوگوں پر ڈاﻻ تھا، اے ہمارے رب! ہم پر وه بوجھ نہ ڈال جس کی ہمیں طاقت نہ ہو اور ہم سے درگزر فرما! اور ہمیں بخش دے اور ہم پر رحم کر! تو ہی ہمارا مالک ہے، ہمیں کافروں کی قوم پر غلبہ عطا فرما

English Translation:

“Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity. It will have [the consequence of] what [good] it has gained, and it will bear [the consequence of] what [evil] it has earned. "Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we have forgotten or erred. Our Lord, and lay not upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord, and burden us not with that which we have no ability to bear. And pardon us; and forgive us; and have mercy upon us. You are our protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He does not charge a soul except with what is within its capacity.

Each person will have the consequence of what good they earn and what they do of harm—and the believers pray for ease, forgiveness, and help against the disbelieving people.

You are not asked to carry what Allah knows you cannot bear.

Reflection

The burden on you was measured by the One who made you.

Ask Him for ease, pardon, and help—He knows your limits.

Reference: Quran 2:286, Surah Al-Baqara.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6369

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

Urdu Translation:

اے اللہ! میں تیری پناہ مانگتا ہوں غم و الم سے، عاجزی سے، سستی سے، بزدلی سے، بخل، قرض چڑھ جانے اور لوگوں کے غلبہ سے

English Translation:

“O Allah! I seek refuge with You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from being heavily in debt and from being overpowered by (other) men.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge in Allah from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from crushing debt and being overpowered by people.

This dua shows that emotional burdens, spiritual stagnation, and worldly pressures are all things we may bring to Allah.

Even the Messenger ﷺ modeled asking for protection from what weighs on the heart.

Reflection

You are allowed to name your fears before Allah.

Seek His refuge from worry, grief, and whatever leaves you feeling small or stuck.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369, Book of Invocations.

Sunan Ibn Majah 3882

Book 34: Supplication (كتاب الدعاء)

It was narrated that Asma’ bint ‘Umais said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

عَلَّمَنِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ كَلِمَاتٍ أَقُولُهُنَّ عِنْدَ الْكَرْبِ اللَّهُ اللَّهُ رَبِّي لاَ أُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا

Urdu Translation:

رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم نے مجھے یہ کلمات سکھائے کہ میں سخت تکلیف کے وقت پڑھا کروں، ( وہ کلمات یہ ہیں ) «الله الله ربي لا أشرك به شيئا» اللہ، اللہ ہی میرا رب ہے میں اس کے ساتھ کسی کو شریک نہیں ٹھہراتی

English Translation:

“The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) taught me some words to say at times of distress: Allah! Allahu Rabbi la ushriku bihi shay’an (Allah, Allah is my Lord, I do not associate anything with Him)”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ taught Asma bint 'Umays these words for moments of distress: Allah, Allah is my Lord; I associate nothing with Him.

In the middle of worry, the heart returns to its simplest truth — who its Lord is.

Reflection

When your thoughts race, slow down and say it: Allahu Allahu Rabbi, la ushriku bihi shay'an.

A few words of tawhid can steady a restless heart.

Reference: Sunan Ibn Majah 3882, Book of Supplication.
