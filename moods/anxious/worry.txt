Quran 3:154

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡغَمِّ أَمَنَةࣰ نُّعَاسࣰ ا يَغۡشَىٰ طَآئِفَةࣰ مِّنكُمۡۖ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ قَدۡ أَهَمَّتۡهُمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ يَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ظَنَّ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِۖ يَقُولُونَ هَل لَّنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِن شَيۡءࣲۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ كُلَّهُۥ لِلَّهِۗ يُخۡفُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِم مَّا لَا يُبۡدُونَ لَكَۖ يَقُولُونَ لَوۡ كَانَ لَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ شَيۡءࣱ مَّا قُتِلۡنَا هَٰهُنَاۗ قُل لَّوۡ كُنتُمۡ فِي بُيُوتِكُمۡ لَبَرَزَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَتۡلُ إِلَىٰ مَضَاجِعِهِمۡۖ وَلِيَبۡتَلِيَ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ وَلِيُمَحِّصَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

English Translation:

“Then after distress, He sent down upon you security [in the form of] drowsiness, overcoming a faction of you, while another faction worried about themselves, thinking of Allah other than the truth - the thought of ignorance, saying, "Is there anything for us [to have done] in this matter?" Say, "Indeed, the matter belongs completely to Allah." They conceal within themselves what they will not reveal to you. They say, "If there was anything we could have done in the matter, some of us would not have been killed right here." Say, "Even if you had been inside your houses, those decreed to be killed would have come out to their death beds." [It was] so that Allah might test what is in your breasts and purify what is in your hearts. And Allah is Knowing of that within the breasts”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah recalls how after distress Allah sent down security as drowsiness upon some, while others were troubled by their own assumptions—thinking thoughts of the pre-Islamic ignorance—saying, “Do we have any say in the matter?”

Allah reminds that all matters belong to Him, and He tests what is in the chests.

Security after fear is a gift; anxious suspicion is a trial of the heart.

Reflection

When fear passes, recognize the calm as from Allah.

Hand the matter back to Him—He owns every outcome.

Reference: Quran 3:154, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 65:3

Surah At-Talaaq (سُورَةُ الطَّلَاقِ)

Arabic:

وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدۡرࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us He will provide from where one does not expect—and whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient. Allah will accomplish His purpose, and He has set a measure for everything.

Tawakkul meets unexpected provision and the sufficiency of Allah Himself.

His purpose is never frustrated.

Reflection

Rely on Allah—He is enough, and provision may arrive from unseen directions.

His purpose will be fulfilled in its measured time.

Reference: Quran 65:3, Surah At-Talaaq.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6369

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

English Translation:

“O Allah! I seek refuge with You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from being heavily in debt and from being overpowered by (other) men.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge in Allah from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from crushing debt and being overpowered by people.

This dua shows that emotional burdens, spiritual stagnation, and worldly pressures are all things we may bring to Allah.

Even the Messenger ﷺ modeled asking for protection from what weighs on the heart.

Reflection

You are allowed to name your fears before Allah.

Seek His refuge from worry, grief, and whatever leaves you feeling small or stuck.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369, Book of Invocations.

Sunan Abi Dawud 1518

Book 8: Prayer (Kitab Al-Salat): Detailed Injunctions about Witr (كتاب الوتر)

Narrated Abdullah ibn Abbas (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

مَنْ لَزِمَ الاِسْتِغْفَارَ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ مِنْ كُلِّ ضِيقٍ مَخْرَجًا وَمِنْ كُلِّ هَمٍّ فَرَجًا وَرَزَقَهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لاَ يَحْتَسِبُ

English Translation:

“If anyone continually asks pardon, Allah will appoint for him a way out of every distress, and a relief from every anxiety, and will provide for him from where he did not reckon.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that continually seeking Allah’s forgiveness opens doors we may not see.

Istighfar is not only for after a mistake; it is a habit that softens hardship. Allah promises a way out of distress, relief from anxiety, and provision from places we never expected.

When life feels closed in, returning to Allah again and again is itself a path toward ease.

Reflection

Keep asking Allah to forgive you—even when you feel stuck.

Relief and provision often follow hearts that turn back to Him regularly.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 1518, Book of Prayer (Kitab Al-Salat): Detailed Injunctions about Witr.

Sunan an-Nasa'i 5453

Book 50: The Book of Seeking Refuge with Allah (كتاب الاستعاذة)

It was narrated from Anas bin Malik, that  (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

English Translation:

“It was narrated from Anas bin Malik, that :When the Messenger of Allah [SAW] supplicated, he would say: "Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi, wal-hazani, wal-'ajzi, wal-kasali, wal-bukhli, wal-jubni, wad-dala'id-daini, wa ghalabatir-rijal (O Allah, I seek refuge with You from worry, grief, incapacity, laziness, miserliness, cowardice, difficult debt and being overpowered by men).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ would seek refuge in Allah from worry, grief, incapacity, laziness, miserliness, cowardice, the burden of debt, and being overpowered by people.

This dua gathers the quiet struggles many carry—emotional, spiritual, and worldly—and places them before Allah.

It is prophetic permission to ask for relief from what drains strength.

Reflection

Bring your worry and heaviness to Allah by name.

Seeking His refuge from grief and incapacity is sunnah, not weakness.

Reference: Sunan an-Nasa'i 5453, Book of The Book of Seeking Refuge with Allah.
