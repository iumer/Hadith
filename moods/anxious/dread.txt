Quran 21:103

Surah Al-Anbiyaa (سُورَةُ الأَنبِيَاءِ)

Arabic:

لَا يَحۡزُنُهُمُ ٱلۡفَزَعُ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرُ وَتَتَلَقَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ هَٰذَا يَوۡمُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ

English Translation:

“They will not be grieved by the greatest terror, and the angels will meet them, [saying], "This is your Day which you have been promised”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the righteous will not be grieved by the greatest terror, and angels will meet them with glad tidings of the Day they were promised.

What frightens the world will not overwhelm those who lived for Allah.

A life of faith prepares a gentle meeting at the hardest hour.

Reflection

Live today in a way that makes that Day a greeting, not a terror.

Allah can spare believing hearts from the grief of the greatest fear.

Reference: Quran 21:103, Surah Al-Anbiyaa.

Quran 76:10

Surah Al-Insaan (سُورَةُ الإِنسَانِ)

Arabic:

إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوۡمًا عَبُوسࣰ ا قَمۡطَرِيرࣰ ا

English Translation:

“Indeed, We fear from our Lord a Day austere and distressful”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah voices the righteous: they fear from their Lord a Day that is austere and distressful.

Healthy fear of the Last Day softens arrogance and fuels sincere deeds.

Awe of that Day is part of a living conscience before Allah.

Reflection

Let awareness of that Day keep your heart humble and sincere.

Fearing Allah’s austere Day can protect you from careless living.

Reference: Quran 76:10, Surah Al-Insaan.

Quran 3:175

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ يُخَوِّفُ أَوۡلِيَآءَهُۥ فَلَا تَخَافُوهُمۡ وَخَافُونِ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

English Translation:

“That is only Satan who frightens [you] of his supporters. So fear them not, but fear Me, if you are [indeed] believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that it is only Satan who frightens you of his supporters—so do not fear them, but fear Me, if you are believers.

Fear is sometimes inflated by whispers that magnify people and shrink trust in Allah.

The believer’s awe is redirected toward Allah alone.

Reflection

Do not let Satan inflate your fear of people.

Keep your reverence for Allah, and watch lesser fears lose their grip.

Reference: Quran 3:175, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.
