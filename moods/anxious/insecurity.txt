Quran 95:4

Surah At-Tin (سُورَةُ التِّينِ)

Arabic:

لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمࣲ‏

Urdu Translation:

یقیناً ہم نے انسان کو بہترین صورت میں پیدا کیا

English Translation:

“We have certainly created man in the best of stature”

Meaning ❤️

Allah declares that He created the human being in the best of forms.

Your worth was set by your Creator before anyone else had an opinion.

Reflection

You were made in the best of forms.

Let that speak louder than your insecurities.

Reference: Quran 95:4, Surah At-Tin.

Quran 9:51

Surah At-Tawba (سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ)

Arabic:

قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Urdu Translation:

آپ کہہ دیجئے کہ ہمیں سوائے اللہ کے ہمارے حق میں لکھے ہوئے کہ کوئی چیز پہنچ ہی نہیں سکتی وه ہمارا کار ساز اور مولیٰ ہے۔ مومنوں کو تو اللہ کی ذات پاک پر ہی بھروسہ کرنا چاہئے۔

English Translation:

“Say, "Never will we be struck except by what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector." And upon Allah let the believers rely”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that believers should say they will never be struck except by what He has decreed—He is their protector—and upon Allah the believers rely.

Qadar and wilayah meet: nothing reaches you outside His decree, and He is the guardian you trust.

Tawakkul rests on that twin truth.

Reflection

Nothing hits you except what Allah decreed—and He is your protector.

Rely on Him with a settled heart.

Reference: Quran 9:51, Surah At-Tawba.

Sahih Muslim 2564c

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لاَ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ وَأَمْوَالِكُمْ وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ تمہاری صورتوں اور تمہارے اموال کی طرف نہیں دیکھتا ، لیکن وہ تمہارے دلوں اور اعمال کی طرف دیکھتا ہے

English Translation:

“Verily Allah does not look to your faces and your wealth but He looks to your heart and to your deeds.”

Meaning ❤️

Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth; He looks at your hearts and your deeds.

Insecurity often grows from measuring ourselves by things Allah does not measure.

Reflection

You are not your appearance or your bank balance.

Work on your heart — that is what He sees.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2564c, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2517

Book 37: Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara' (كتاب صفة القيامة والرقائق والورع عن رسول الله صلى الله)

Anas bin Malik narrated that a man said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَعْقِلُهَا وَأَتَوَكَّلُ أَوْ أُطْلِقُهَا وَأَتَوَكَّلُ قَالَ اعْقِلْهَا وَتَوَكَّلْ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ کے رسول! کیا میں اونٹ کو پہلے باندھ دوں پھر اللہ پر توکل کروں یا چھوڑ دوں پھر توکل کروں؟ آپ نے فرمایا: ”اسے باندھ دو، پھر توکل کرو

English Translation:

“O Messenger of Allah! Shall I tie it and rely(upon Allah), or leave it loose and rely(upon Allah)?" He said: "Tie it and rely(upon Allah)”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ was asked whether to tie the animal and rely on Allah, or leave it and rely—he said: tie it and rely upon Allah.

Tawakkul joins practical precaution with trust in the Guarantor.

Faith is not neglect dressed as spirituality.

Reflection

Take the means, then trust Allah with the outcome.

Tie the rope and rely—both together are prophetic wisdom.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2517, Book of Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara'.
