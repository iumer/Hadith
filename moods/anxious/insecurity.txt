Quran 3:173

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدۡ جَمَعُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱخۡشَوۡهُمۡ فَزَادَهُمۡ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡوَكِيلُ

Urdu Translation:

وه لوگ کہ جب ان سے لوگوں نے کہا کہ کافروں نے تمہارے مقابلے پر لشکر جمع کر لئے ہیں، تم ان سے خوف کھاؤ تو اس بات نے انہیں ایمان میں اور بڑھا دیا اور کہنے لگے ہمیں اللہ کافی ہے اور وه بہت اچھا کارساز ہے

English Translation:

“Those to whom hypocrites said, "Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them." But it [merely] increased them in faith, and they said, "Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of believers who, when told that people had gathered against them, only increased in faith and said, “Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.”

Threats became fuel for trust rather than collapse.

Hasbunallahu wa ni‘mal wakeel is a refuge in frightening news.

Reflection

When fear-mongering arrives, answer with: Allah is sufficient for us.

Let intimidation increase your trust, not your panic.

Reference: Quran 3:173, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 9:51

Surah At-Tawba (سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ)

Arabic:

قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ

Urdu Translation:

آپ کہہ دیجئے کہ ہمیں سوائے اللہ کے ہمارے حق میں لکھے ہوئے کہ کوئی چیز پہنچ ہی نہیں سکتی وه ہمارا کار ساز اور مولیٰ ہے۔ مومنوں کو تو اللہ کی ذات پاک پر ہی بھروسہ کرنا چاہئے۔

English Translation:

“Say, "Never will we be struck except by what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector." And upon Allah let the believers rely”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that believers should say they will never be struck except by what He has decreed—He is their protector—and upon Allah the believers rely.

Qadar and wilayah meet: nothing reaches you outside His decree, and He is the guardian you trust.

Tawakkul rests on that twin truth.

Reflection

Nothing hits you except what Allah decreed—and He is your protector.

Rely on Him with a settled heart.

Reference: Quran 9:51, Surah At-Tawba.

Quran 65:3

Surah At-Talaaq (سُورَةُ الطَّلَاقِ)

Arabic:

وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدۡرࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

اور اسے ایسی جگہ سے روزی دیتا ہے جس کا اسے گمان بھی نہ ہو اور جو شخص اللہ پر توکل کرے گا اللہ اسے کافی ہوگا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ اپنا کام پورا کرکے ہی رہے گا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ نے ہر چیز کا ایک اندازه مقرر کر رکھا ہے

English Translation:

“And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us He will provide from where one does not expect—and whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient. Allah will accomplish His purpose, and He has set a measure for everything.

Tawakkul meets unexpected provision and the sufficiency of Allah Himself.

His purpose is never frustrated.

Reflection

Rely on Allah—He is enough, and provision may arrive from unseen directions.

His purpose will be fulfilled in its measured time.

Reference: Quran 65:3, Surah At-Talaaq.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2344

Book 36: Chapters On Zuhd (كتاب الزهد عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

It is narrated:

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَوْ أَنَّكُمْ كُنْتُمْ تَوَكَّلُونَ عَلَى اللَّهِ حَقَّ تَوَكُّلِهِ لَرُزِقْتُمْ كَمَا تُرْزَقُ الطَّيْرُ تَغْدُو خِمَاصًا وَتَرُوحُ بِطَانًا

Urdu Translation:

اگر تم لوگ اللہ پر توکل ( بھروسہ ) کرو جیسا کہ اس پر توکل ( بھروسہ ) کرنے کا حق ہے تو تمہیں اسی طرح رزق ملے گا جیسا کہ پرندوں کو ملتا ہے کہ صبح کو وہ بھوکے نکلتے ہیں اور شام کو آسودہ واپس آتے ہیں

English Translation:

“If you were to rely upon Allah with the required reliance, then He would provide for you just as a bird is provided for, it goes out in the morning empty, and returns full.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that if we relied upon Allah with true reliance, He would provide for us as He provides for birds—they leave hungry in the morning and return full.

Tawakkul includes going out and trusting the Provider, not sitting still in despair.

Provision meets movement wrapped in trust.

Reflection

Rely on Allah like the bird that still flies out each morning.

Trust does not cancel effort; it frees effort from panic.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2344, Book of Chapters On Zuhd.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2516

Book 37: Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara' (كتاب صفة القيامة والرقائق والورع عن رسول الله صلى الله)

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas narrated (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَا غُلاَمُ إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الأُمَّةَ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلاَّ بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ لَكَ وَلَوِ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ إِلاَّ بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكَ رُفِعَتِ الأَقْلاَمُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُفُ

Urdu Translation:

اے لڑکے! بیشک میں تمہیں چند اہم باتیں بتلا رہا ہوں: تم اللہ کے احکام کی حفاظت کرو، وہ تمہاری حفاظت فرمائے گا، تو اللہ کے حقوق کا خیال رکھو اسے تم اپنے سامنے پاؤ گے، جب تم کوئی چیز مانگو تو صرف اللہ سے مانگو، جب تو مدد چاہو تو صرف اللہ سے مدد طلب کرو، اور یہ بات جان لو کہ اگر ساری امت بھی جمع ہو کر تمہیں کچھ نفع پہنچانا چاہے تو وہ تمہیں اس سے زیادہ کچھ بھی نفع نہیں پہنچا سکتی جو اللہ نے تمہارے لیے لکھ دیا ہے، اور اگر وہ تمہیں کچھ نقصان پہنچانے کے لیے جمع ہو جائے تو اس سے زیادہ کچھ نقصان نہیں پہنچا سکتی جو اللہ نے تمہارے لیے لکھ دیا ہے، قلم اٹھا لیے گئے اور ( تقدیر کے ) صحیفے خشک ہو گئے ہیں

English Translation:

“O boy! I will teach you a statement: Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you. Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him before you. When you ask, ask Allah, and when you seek aid, seek Allah's aid. Know that if the entire creation were to gather together to do something to benefit you- you would never get any benefit except that Allah had written for you. And if they were to gather to do something to harm you- you would never be harmed except that Allah had written for you. The pens are lifted and the pages are dried.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches: be mindful of Allah and He will protect you; be mindful of Him and you will find Him before you. Ask Allah and seek His aid. If all creation gathered to benefit or harm you, they could only do what He has written.

The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried.

Mindfulness of Allah brings protection and clarity about decree.

Reflection

Keep Allah before you in your choices, and seek help from Him alone.

What was written will reach you—walk with mindfulness, not fear of creation.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2516, Book of Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara'.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2517

Book 37: Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara' (كتاب صفة القيامة والرقائق والورع عن رسول الله صلى الله)

Anas bin Malik narrated that a man said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اعْقِلْهَا وَتَوَكَّلْ

Urdu Translation:

اسے باندھ دو، پھر توکل کرو

English Translation:

“Tie it and rely(upon Allah)”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ was asked whether to tie the animal and rely on Allah, or leave it and rely—he said: tie it and rely upon Allah.

Tawakkul joins practical precaution with trust in the Guarantor.

Faith is not neglect dressed as spirituality.

Reflection

Take the means, then trust Allah with the outcome.

Tie the rope and rely—both together are prophetic wisdom.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2517, Book of Chapters on the description of the Day of Judgement, Ar-Riqaq, and Al-Wara'.
