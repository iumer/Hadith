Quran 4:148

Surah An-Nisaa (سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ)

Arabic:

۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ سَمِيعًا عَلِيمًا

English Translation:

“Allah does not like the public mention of evil except by one who has been wronged. And ever is Allah Hearing and Knowing”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He does not like the public mention of evil except by one who has been wronged—and He is ever Hearing and Knowing.

Broadcasting harm is disliked; the oppressed may speak of the injustice done to them.

Allah hears both the wound and the speech about it.

Reflection

Do not spread evil talk without cause.

If you have been wronged, know that Allah hears and knows your pain.

Reference: Quran 4:148, Surah An-Nisaa.

Quran 4:149

Surah An-Nisaa (سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ)

Arabic:

إِن تُبۡدُواْ خَيۡرًا أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ أَوۡ تَعۡفُواْ عَن سُوٓءࣲ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوࣰّ ا قَدِيرًا

English Translation:

“If [instead] you show [some] good or conceal it or pardon an offense - indeed, Allah is ever Pardoning and Competent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that if you show some good, conceal it, or pardon an offense—indeed, Allah is ever Pardoning and Competent.

Public good, private good, and forgiveness are all beloved paths.

Pardon mirrors a quality of the One who pardons with full power.

Reflection

Choose to show good, hide good, or forgive harm—Allah loves these.

Pardon others, remembering how Pardoning He is.

Reference: Quran 4:149, Surah An-Nisaa.

Quran 16:90

Surah An-Nahl (سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ)

Arabic:

۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

English Translation:

“Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives and forbids immorality and bad conduct and oppression. He admonishes you that perhaps you will be reminded”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He orders justice, good conduct, and giving to relatives, and forbids immorality, bad conduct, and oppression.

This ayah sketches a moral compass for private and public life: fairness, kindness, and generosity—while rejecting harm and injustice.

Living by these commands brings beauty to character and community.

Reflection

Let justice and kindness shape your daily choices.

What Allah commands leads hearts and societies toward wholeness.

Reference: Quran 16:90, Surah An-Nahl.

Sahih al-Bukhari 2443

Book 46: Oppressions (كتاب المظالم)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

انْصُرْ أَخَاكَ ظَالِمًا أَوْ مَظْلُومًا

English Translation:

“Help your brother, whether he is an oppressor or he is an oppressed one.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that we should help our brother whether he is oppressed or oppressing.

Helping the oppressed means standing with justice. Helping the oppressor means stopping him from wrongdoing—so that he does not continue harm.

True support is not always agreement; sometimes it is loving prevention of sin.

Reflection

Help can mean defending someone—or gently restraining them from harm.

Both are forms of care rooted in justice.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 2443, Book of Oppressions.

Sahih al-Bukhari 2447

Book 46: Oppressions (كتاب المظالم)

Narrated Ibn `Umar (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

الظُّلْمُ ظُلُمَاتٌ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ

English Translation:

“Oppression will be a darkness on the Day of Resurrection.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ warns that oppression will appear as darkness on the Day of Resurrection.

Wronging others—whether through words, power, or neglect—does not disappear; it returns as a heavy burden in the next life.

This hadith calls us to examine how we treat people while we still have time to make things right.

Reflection

Every act of injustice casts a shadow that follows into the Hereafter.

Choose fairness today so your meeting with Allah is not darkened by harm you caused.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 2447, Book of Oppressions.

Sahih Muslim 2578

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Jabir b. Abdullah reported that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اتَّقُوا الظُّلْمَ فَإِنَّ الظُّلْمَ ظُلُمَاتٌ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ وَاتَّقُوا الشُّحَّ فَإِنَّ الشُّحَّ أَهْلَكَ مَنْ كَانَ قَبْلَكُمْ حَمَلَهُمْ عَلَى أَنْ سَفَكُوا دِمَاءَهُمْ وَاسْتَحَلُّوا مَحَارِمَهُمْ

English Translation:

“Be on your guard against committing oppression, for oppression is a darkness on the Day of Resurrection, and be on your guard against stinginess for stinginess destroyed those who were before you, as it incited them to shed blood and make lawful what was unlawful for them.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ warns us to guard against oppression, for it will be darkness on the Day of Resurrection.

He also warns against stinginess, which destroyed earlier peoples by pushing them toward bloodshed and making the unlawful seem lawful.

Justice and generosity protect both society and the soul.

Reflection

Refuse the darkness of wronging others.

Generosity and fairness keep the heart from paths that destroy nations—and individuals.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2578, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2014

Book 27: Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives (كتاب البر والصلة عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas narrated (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اتَّقِ دَعْوَةَ الْمَظْلُومِ فَإِنَّهَا لَيْسَ بَيْنَهَا وَبَيْنَ اللَّهِ حِجَابٌ

English Translation:

“'Beware of the supplication of the oppressed; for indeed there is no barrier between it and Allah.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ warns to beware the supplication of the oppressed—for there is no barrier between it and Allah.

The dua of someone wronged rises freely to Him.

This should make us careful never to become the cause of such a prayer against us.

Reflection

Never take lightly the pain of someone you might oppress.

Their cry to Allah travels with no barrier.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2014, Book of Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives.
