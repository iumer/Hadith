Quran 4:148-149

Surah An-Nisaa (سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ)

Arabic:

۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ سَمِيعًا عَلِيمًا ﴿١٤٨﴾ إِن تُبۡدُواْ خَيۡرًا أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ أَوۡ تَعۡفُواْ عَن سُوٓءࣲ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوࣰّ ا قَدِيرًا ﴿١٤٩﴾

Urdu Translation:

(148) برائی کے ساتھ آواز بلند کرنے کو اللہ تعالیٰ پسند نہیں فرماتا مگر مظلوم کی اجازت ہے اور اللہ تعالیٰ خوب سنتا جانتا ہے (149) اگر تم کسی نیکی کو علانیہ کرو یا پوشیده، یا کسی برائی سے درگزر کرو، پس یقیناً اللہ تعالیٰ پوری معافی کرنے واﻻ اور پوری قدرت واﻻ ہے

English Translation:

“(148) Allah does not like the public mention of evil except by one who has been wronged. And ever is Allah Hearing and Knowing (149) If [instead] you show [some] good or conceal it or pardon an offense - indeed, Allah is ever Pardoning and Competent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah does not like the public mention of evil, except by one who has been wronged — Allah hears and knows. Yet if you show good, hide it, or pardon a wrong, Allah is Pardoning and All-Powerful.

The wronged person is allowed to speak up — and forgiving is held up as the nobler path.

Reflection

Your hurt is heard by Allah.

If you can pardon, you are imitating the One who pardons.

Reference: Quran 4:148-149, Surah An-Nisaa.

Quran 14:42

Surah Ibrahim (سُورَةُ إِبۡرَاهِيمَ)

Arabic:

وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَٰفِلًا عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَۚ إِنَّمَا يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ لِيَوۡمࣲ تَشۡخَصُ فِيهِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ

Urdu Translation:

ناانصافوں کے اعمال سے اللہ کو غافل نہ سمجھ وه تو انہیں اس دن تک مہلت دیے ہوئے ہے جس دن آنکھیں پھٹی کی پھٹی ره جائیں گی

English Translation:

“And never think that Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do. He only delays them for a Day when eyes will stare [in horror]”

Meaning ❤️

Allah addresses the one who has been wronged: never think Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do. He only delays them to a Day when eyes will stare in horror.

The delay is not neglect.

Reflection

Allah sees what was done to you.

Leave the account with Him; it is in safe hands.

Reference: Quran 14:42, Surah Ibrahim.

Quran 16:90

Surah An-Nahl (سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ)

Arabic:

۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ عدل کا، بھلائی کا اور قرابت داروں کے ساتھ سلوک کرنے کا حکم دیتا ہے اور بےحیائی کے کاموں، ناشائستہ حرکتوں اور ﻇلم وزیادتی سے روکتا ہے، وه خود تمہیں نصیحتیں کر رہا ہے کہ تم نصیحت حاصل کرو

English Translation:

“Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives and forbids immorality and bad conduct and oppression. He admonishes you that perhaps you will be reminded”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He orders justice, good conduct, and giving to relatives, and forbids immorality, bad conduct, and oppression.

This ayah sketches a moral compass for private and public life: fairness, kindness, and generosity—while rejecting harm and injustice.

Living by these commands brings beauty to character and community.

Reflection

Let justice and kindness shape your daily choices.

What Allah commands leads hearts and societies toward wholeness.

Reference: Quran 16:90, Surah An-Nahl.

Sahih Muslim 2582

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَتُؤَدُّنَّ الْحُقُوقَ إِلَى أَهْلِهَا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ حَتَّى يُقَادَ لِلشَّاةِ الْجَلْحَاءِ مِنَ الشَّاةِ الْقَرْنَاءِ

Urdu Translation:

قیامت کے دن تم سب حقداروں کے حقوق ان کو ادا کرو گے ، حتی کہ اس بکری کا بدلہ بھی جس کے سینگ توڑ دیے گئے ہوں گے ، سینگوں والی بکری سے پورا پورا لیا جائے گا

English Translation:

“Rights will certainly be restored to those entitled to them on the Day of Resurrection, (to the point that) even the hornless sheep will lay claim upon the horned one.”

Meaning ❤️

Every right will be returned to its owner on the Day of Resurrection — even the hornless sheep will get justice from the horned one.

No wrong is forgotten.

Reflection

You don't have to win every battle here to be made whole.

Allah's justice misses nothing.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2582, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Sahih al-Bukhari 2447

Book 46: Oppressions (كتاب المظالم)

Narrated Ibn `Umar (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

الظُّلْمُ ظُلُمَاتٌ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ

Urdu Translation:

ظلم قیامت کے دن اندھیرے ہوں گے

English Translation:

“Oppression will be a darkness on the Day of Resurrection.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ warns that oppression will appear as darkness on the Day of Resurrection.

Wronging others—whether through words, power, or neglect—does not disappear; it returns as a heavy burden in the next life.

This hadith calls us to examine how we treat people while we still have time to make things right.

Reflection

Every act of injustice casts a shadow that follows into the Hereafter.

Choose fairness today so your meeting with Allah is not darkened by harm you caused.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 2447, Book of Oppressions.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2014

Book 27: Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives (كتاب البر والصلة عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Narrated Ibn 'Abbas narrated (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اتَّقِ دَعْوَةَ الْمَظْلُومِ فَإِنَّهَا لَيْسَ بَيْنَهَا وَبَيْنَ اللَّهِ حِجَابٌ

Urdu Translation:

مظلوم کی دعا سے ڈرو، اس لیے کہ اس کے اور اللہ کے درمیان کوئی پردہ آڑے نہیں آتا

English Translation:

“Beware of the supplication of the oppressed; for indeed there is no barrier between it and Allah.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ warns to beware the supplication of the oppressed—for there is no barrier between it and Allah.

The dua of someone wronged rises freely to Him.

This should make us careful never to become the cause of such a prayer against us.

Reflection

Never take lightly the pain of someone you might oppress.

Their cry to Allah travels with no barrier.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2014, Book of Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives.
