Quran 7:199

Surah Al-A'raaf (سُورَةُ الأَعۡرَافِ)

Arabic:

خُذِ ٱلۡعَفۡوَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡعُرۡفِ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ

English Translation:

“Take what is given freely, enjoin what is good, and turn away from the ignorant”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to take what is given freely, enjoin what is good, and turn away from the ignorant.

Accept ease when it comes, promote good, and do not descend into every ignorant quarrel.

Wisdom knows when to engage and when to gracefully disengage.

Reflection

Take the easy good, promote what is right, and step away from ignorance.

Not every provocation deserves your energy.

Reference: Quran 7:199, Surah Al-A'raaf.

Quran 25:63

Surah Al-Furqaan (سُورَةُ الفُرۡقَانِ)

Arabic:

وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنࣰ ا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk upon the earth easily, and when the ignorant address them [harshly], they say [words of] peace”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk gently on the earth, and when the ignorant address them harshly, they reply with words of peace.

Dignity here is quiet: humility in gait and calm in speech.

Responding to ignorance with salam protects the heart from being dragged into ugliness.

Reflection

Walk softly and answer harshness with peace.

That gentleness is a mark of those who serve the Most Merciful.

Reference: Quran 25:63, Surah Al-Furqaan.

Quran 73:10

Surah Al-Muzzammil (سُورَةُ المُزَّمِّلِ)

Arabic:

وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱهۡجُرۡهُمۡ هَجۡرࣰ ا جَمِيلࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And be patient over what they say and avoid them with gracious avoidance”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He instructed the Prophet ﷺ to be patient over what they say and to avoid them with gracious avoidance.

Patience with hurtful speech, and dignified distance without ugliness, is prophetic conduct.

You need not absorb every insult to prove strength.

Reflection

Be patient with harsh words, and step back with grace.

Gracious avoidance can protect the heart without bitterness.

Reference: Quran 73:10, Surah Al-Muzzammil.

Al-Adab Al-Mufrad 245

Al-Adab Al-Mufrad (الأدب المفرد)

Ibn 'Abbas reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

علموا ويسروا ولا تعسروا، وإذا غضب أحدكم فليسكت

English Translation:

“Teach and make things easy and not difficult. When one of you is angry, he should be silent.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches us to make learning and guidance easy, not harsh or overwhelming.

He also teaches that when anger comes, silence is a form of protection. Words spoken in rage are often words we regret.

Gentleness in teaching and restraint in anger both reflect a heart seeking Allah’s pleasure.

Reflection

Ease is part of prophetic character.

If anger floods you, choose silence until your heart softens again.

Reference: Al-Adab Al-Mufrad 245, Book of Al-Adab Al-Mufrad.

Sunan Abi Dawud 4782

Book 43: General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab) (كتاب الأدب)

Narrated AbuDharr (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِذَا غَضِبَ أَحَدُكُمْ وَهُوَ قَائِمٌ فَلْيَجْلِسْ فَإِنْ ذَهَبَ عَنْهُ الْغَضَبُ وَإِلاَّ فَلْيَضْطَجِعْ

English Translation:

“When one of you becomes angry while standing, let him sit down. If the anger leaves him, well and good; otherwise let him lie down.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ gives a practical remedy for anger: if you are standing, sit down; if anger remains, lie down.

Changing posture interrupts the body’s surge and creates space to cool down before harm is done.

Faith includes learning how to manage strong emotions with wisdom.

Reflection

When anger rises, change your position before you change someone’s day.

A small physical step can protect hearts—including your own.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 4782, Book of General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab).
