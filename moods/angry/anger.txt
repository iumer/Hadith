Quran 3:133-134

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

۞وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةࣲ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ ﴿١٣٣﴾ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي ٱلسَّرَّآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَٱلۡكَٰظِمِينَ ٱلۡغَيۡظَ وَٱلۡعَافِينَ عَنِ ٱلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ ﴿١٣٤﴾

Urdu Translation:

(133) اور اپنے رب کی بخشش کی طرف اور اس جنت کی طرف دوڑو جس کا عرض آسمانوں اور زمین کے برابر ہے، جو پرہیزگاروں کے لئے تیار کی گئی ہے (134) جو لوگ آسانی میں سختی کے موقع پر بھی اللہ کے راستے میں خرچ کرتے ہیں، غصہ پینے والے اور لوگوں سے درگزر کرنے والے ہیں، اللہ تعالیٰ ان نیک کاروں سے محبت کرتا ہے

English Translation:

“(133) And hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a garden as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous (134) Who spend [in the cause of Allah] during ease and hardship and who restrain anger and who pardon the people - and Allah loves the doers of good”

Meaning ❤️

Allah calls us to hasten to His forgiveness and a Garden as wide as the heavens and the earth, prepared for the righteous — those who give in ease and hardship, who swallow their anger, and who pardon people. And Allah loves those who do good.

Reflection

Restraining anger is a sign of the people of Paradise.

Pause, swallow it, and pardon — Allah loves that.

Reference: Quran 3:133-134, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 42:36-37

Surah Ash-Shura (سُورَةُ الشُّورَىٰ)

Arabic:

فَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّن شَيۡءࣲ فَمَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ ﴿٣٦﴾ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ وَإِذَا مَا غَضِبُواْ هُمۡ يَغۡفِرُونَ ﴿٣٧﴾

Urdu Translation:

(36) تو تمہیں جو کچھ دیا گیا ہے وه زندگانی دنیا کا کچھ یونہی سا اسباب ہے، اور اللہ کے پاس جو ہے وه اس سے بدرجہ بہتر اور پائیدار ہے، وه ان کے لیے ہے جو ایمان ﻻئے اور صرف اپنے رب ہی پر بھروسہ رکھتے ہیں (37) اور کبیره گناہوں سے اور بے حیائیوں سے بچتے ہیں اور غصے کے وقت (بھی) معاف کردیتے ہیں

English Translation:

“(36) So whatever thing you have been given - it is but [for] enjoyment of the worldly life. But what is with Allah is better and more lasting for those who have believed and upon their Lord rely (37) And those who avoid the major sins and immoralities, and when they are angry, they forgive”

Meaning ❤️

Allah reminds us that worldly things are temporary, while what is with Him is better and lasting — for those who believe, rely on their Lord, avoid major sins, and when angry, forgive.

Forgiving in anger is named among the qualities of those who receive what lasts.

Reflection

Is this anger worth what is lasting?

Forgive, and choose what is with Allah.

Reference: Quran 42:36-37, Surah Ash-Shura.

Quran 41:34

Surah Fussilat (سُورَةُ فُصِّلَتۡ)

Arabic:

وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةࣱ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمࣱ‏

Urdu Translation:

نیکی اور بدی برابر نہیں ہوتی۔ برائی کو بھلائی سے دفع کرو پھر وہی جس کے اور تمہارے درمیان دشمنی ہے ایسا ہو جائے گا جیسے دلی دوست

English Translation:

“And not equal are the good deed and the bad. Repel [evil] by that [deed] which is better; and thereupon the one whom between you and him is enmity [will become] as though he was a devoted friend”

Meaning ❤️

Good and evil are not equal. Repel harm with what is better, and the one who was your enemy may become a close friend.

Anger wants to answer harm with harm; this verse offers a stronger path.

Reflection

Before you reply, ask: what is the better response?

Gentleness can turn an enemy into a friend.

Reference: Quran 41:34, Surah Fussilat.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6114

Book 78: Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab) (كتاب الأدب)

Narrated Abu Huraira (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَيْسَ الشَّدِيدُ بِالصُّرَعَةِ، إِنَّمَا الشَّدِيدُ الَّذِي يَمْلِكُ نَفْسَهُ عِنْدَ الْغَضَبِ

Urdu Translation:

پہلوان وہ نہیں ہے جو کشتی لڑنے میں غالب ہو جائے بلکہ اصلی پہلوان تو وہ ہے جو غصہ کی حالت میں اپنے آپ پر قابو پائے بے قابو نہ ہو جائے

English Translation:

“The strong is not the one who overcomes the people by his strength, but the strong is the one who controls himself while in anger.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ redefines strength: it is not the one who overpowers people, but the one who controls himself when angry.

Physical force can impress others for a moment; self-mastery reshapes character for a lifetime.

The real battlefield of strength is often inside the chest.

Reflection

Your greatest strength may be the anger you choose not to unleash.

Mastering yourself is a victory Allah loves.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6114, Book of Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab).

Sahih Muslim 2610a

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Sulaiman b. Surad reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنِّي لأَعْرِفُ كَلِمَةً لَوْ قَالَهَا لَذَهَبَ عَنْهُ الَّذِي يَجِدُ أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ

Urdu Translation:

مجھے ایک ایسا کلمہ معلوم ہے کہ اگر یہ شخص اسے کہہ دے تو وہ ( غصے کی ) جس کیفیت میں خود کو پا رہا ہے وہ جاتی رہے گی ، ( وہ کلمہ ہے ) : اعوذبالله من الشيطان الرجيم

English Translation:

“I know of a wording, if he were to utter that, his fit of rage (would be no more and that wording is): I seek refuge with Allah from Satan the accursed”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ saw a man’s anger burning in his face and taught a wording that can lift rage: seeking refuge with Allah from Satan the accursed.

Anger can be stirred by whispers that push us toward harm; turning to Allah interrupts that spiral.

A short remembrance can change the course of a heated moment.

Reflection

When anger flares, seek Allah’s protection before you speak.

A brief refuge in Him can cool what threatens to burn.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2610a, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2020

Book 27: Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives (كتاب البر والصلة عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Abu Hurairah narrated that a man came to the Messenger of Allah and said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

عَلِّمْنِي شَيْئًا وَلاَ تُكْثِرْ عَلَىَّ لَعَلِّي أَعِيهِ . قَالَ لاَ تَغْضَبْ . فَرَدَّدَ ذَلِكَ مِرَارًا كُلُّ ذَلِكَ يَقُولُ لاَ تَغْضَبْ

Urdu Translation:

مجھے کچھ سکھائیے لیکن زیادہ نہ بتائیے تاکہ میں اسے یاد رکھ سکوں، آپ نے فرمایا: ”غصہ مت کرو“، وہ کئی بار یہی سوال دہراتا رہا اور آپ ہر بار کہتے رہے ”غصہ مت کرو

English Translation:

“Teach me something that is not too much for me so that, perhaps, I may abide by it." He (S.A.W) said: "Do not get angry." He repeated that (the request) a number of times, each time he replied: 'Do not get angry”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ was asked for advice that would not be too much to keep, and he replied repeatedly: do not get angry.

He kept returning to that single counsel, showing how central anger-management is to a livable faith.

Sometimes the most powerful advice is short and repeated.

Reflection

If you take one practice to heart, let it be: do not get angry.

That simple guard can spare you countless regrets.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2020, Book of Chapters on Righteousness And Maintaining Good Relations With Relatives.

Sunan Abi Dawud 4777

Book 43: General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab) (كتاب الأدب)

Narrated Mu'adh ibn Anas (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

مَنْ كَظَمَ غَيْظًا - وَهُوَ قَادِرٌ عَلَى أَنْ يُنْفِذَهُ - دَعَاهُ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ عَلَى رُءُوسِ الْخَلاَئِقِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ حَتَّى يُخَيِّرَهُ اللَّهُ مِنَ الْحُورِ مَا شَاءَ

Urdu Translation:

جس نے اپنا غصہ پی لیا حالانکہ وہ اسے نافذ کرنے پر قادر تھا تو قیامت کے دن اللہ تعالیٰ اسے سب لوگوں کے سامنے بلائے گا یہاں تک کہ اسے اللہ تعالیٰ اختیار دے گا کہ وہ بڑی آنکھ والی حوروں میں سے جسے چاہے چن لے

English Translation:

“If anyone suppresses anger when he is in a position to give vent to it, Allah, the Exalted, will call him on the Day of Resurrection over the heads of all creatures, and ask him to choose any of the bright and large eyed maidens he wishes”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that restraining anger—especially when you have the power to act on it—is a great act of worship.

Allah will honor such a person on the Day of Resurrection, calling them before creation and granting them a choice of reward.

True strength here is not weakness; it is choosing self-control when reaction would be easy.

Reflection

The moment you could explode but choose restraint, Allah sees it.

That patience may carry a reward far greater than the satisfaction of reacting.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 4777, Book of General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab).

Sunan Abi Dawud 4782

Book 43: General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab) (كتاب الأدب)

Narrated AbuDharr (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِذَا غَضِبَ أَحَدُكُمْ وَهُوَ قَائِمٌ فَلْيَجْلِسْ فَإِنْ ذَهَبَ عَنْهُ الْغَضَبُ وَإِلاَّ فَلْيَضْطَجِعْ

Urdu Translation:

جب تم میں سے کسی کو غصہ آئے اور وہ کھڑا ہو تو چاہیئے کہ بیٹھ جائے، اب اگر اس کا غصہ رفع ہو جائے ( تو بہتر ہے ) ورنہ پھر لیٹ جائے

English Translation:

“When one of you becomes angry while standing, he should sit down. If the anger leaves him, well and good; otherwise he should lie down”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ gives a practical remedy for anger: if you are standing, sit down; if anger remains, lie down.

Changing posture interrupts the body’s surge and creates space to cool down before harm is done.

Faith includes learning how to manage strong emotions with wisdom.

Reflection

When anger rises, change your position before you change someone’s day.

A small physical step can protect hearts—including your own.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 4782, Book of General Behavior (Kitab Al-Adab).
