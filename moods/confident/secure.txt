Quran 3:154

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡغَمِّ أَمَنَةࣰ نُّعَاسࣰ ا يَغۡشَىٰ طَآئِفَةࣰ مِّنكُمۡۖ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ قَدۡ أَهَمَّتۡهُمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ يَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ظَنَّ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِۖ يَقُولُونَ هَل لَّنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِن شَيۡءࣲۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ كُلَّهُۥ لِلَّهِۗ يُخۡفُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِم مَّا لَا يُبۡدُونَ لَكَۖ يَقُولُونَ لَوۡ كَانَ لَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ شَيۡءࣱ مَّا قُتِلۡنَا هَٰهُنَاۗ قُل لَّوۡ كُنتُمۡ فِي بُيُوتِكُمۡ لَبَرَزَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَتۡلُ إِلَىٰ مَضَاجِعِهِمۡۖ وَلِيَبۡتَلِيَ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ وَلِيُمَحِّصَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

English Translation:

“Then after distress, He sent down upon you security [in the form of] drowsiness, overcoming a faction of you, while another faction worried about themselves, thinking of Allah other than the truth - the thought of ignorance, saying, "Is there anything for us [to have done] in this matter?" Say, "Indeed, the matter belongs completely to Allah." They conceal within themselves what they will not reveal to you. They say, "If there was anything we could have done in the matter, some of us would not have been killed right here." Say, "Even if you had been inside your houses, those decreed to be killed would have come out to their death beds." [It was] so that Allah might test what is in your breasts and purify what is in your hearts. And Allah is Knowing of that within the breasts”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah recalls how after distress Allah sent down security as drowsiness upon some, while others were troubled by their own assumptions—thinking thoughts of the pre-Islamic ignorance—saying, “Do we have any say in the matter?”

Allah reminds that all matters belong to Him, and He tests what is in the chests.

Security after fear is a gift; anxious suspicion is a trial of the heart.

Reflection

When fear passes, recognize the calm as from Allah.

Hand the matter back to Him—He owns every outcome.

Reference: Quran 3:154, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 8:11

Surah Al-Anfaal (سُورَةُ الأَنفَالِ)

Arabic:

إِذۡ يُغَشِّيكُمُ ٱلنُّعَاسَ أَمَنَةࣰ مِّنۡهُ وَيُنَزِّلُ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءࣰ لِّيُطَهِّرَكُم بِهِۦ وَيُذۡهِبَ عَنكُمۡ رِجۡزَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ وَلِيَرۡبِطَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتَ بِهِ ٱلۡأَقۡدَامَ

English Translation:

“[Remember] when He overwhelmed you with drowsiness [giving] security from Him and sent down upon you from the sky, rain by which to purify you and remove from you the evil [suggestions] of Satan and to make steadfast your hearts and plant firmly thereby your feet”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us how He covered them with drowsiness as security, sent rain to purify them, remove Satan’s whispers, make hearts steadfast, and plant feet firmly.

Care can arrive as rest, cleansing, and renewed courage.

Allah tends both body and heart in moments of trial.

Reflection

Notice how Allah may send calm and cleansing when you are strained.

Ask Him to steady your heart and firm your feet.

Reference: Quran 8:11, Surah Al-Anfaal.

Quran 9:26

Surah At-Tawba (سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ)

Arabic:

ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَأَنزَلَ جُنُودࣰ ا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَعَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

English Translation:

“Then Allah sent down His tranquillity upon His Messenger and upon the believers and sent down soldiers angels whom you did not see and punished those who disbelieved. And that is the recompense of the disbelievers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us how He sent down His tranquillity upon His Messenger and the believers, and supported them with unseen forces.

Sakinah in crisis is a divine gift that settles hearts while help arrives beyond sight.

Allah’s calm and His soldiers—seen or unseen—aid those He wills.

Reflection

Ask Allah for tranquillity when conflict shakes you.

His unseen support can arrive with the calm He sends.

Reference: Quran 9:26, Surah At-Tawba.

Quran 48:4

Surah Al-Fath (سُورَةُ الفَتۡحِ)

Arabic:

هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡۗ وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمࣰ ا

English Translation:

“It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their [present] faith. And to Allah belong the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and ever is Allah Knowing and Wise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers so that they would increase in faith upon their faith.

Sakinah is a gift that settles the heart and deepens iman.

Calm from Allah is not emptiness—it is faith growing quieter and stronger.

Reflection

Ask Allah to send tranquillity into your heart.

His sakinah increases faith in ways force never could.

Reference: Quran 48:4, Surah Al-Fath.

Quran 65:3

Surah At-Talaaq (سُورَةُ الطَّلَاقِ)

Arabic:

وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدۡرࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us He will provide from where one does not expect—and whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient. Allah will accomplish His purpose, and He has set a measure for everything.

Tawakkul meets unexpected provision and the sufficiency of Allah Himself.

His purpose is never frustrated.

Reflection

Rely on Allah—He is enough, and provision may arrive from unseen directions.

His purpose will be fulfilled in its measured time.

Reference: Quran 65:3, Surah At-Talaaq.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6472

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) (كتاب الرقاق)

Narrated Ibn `Abbas (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مِنْ أُمَّتِي سَبْعُونَ أَلْفًا بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ، هُمُ الَّذِينَ لاَ يَسْتَرْقُونَ، وَلاَ يَتَطَيَّرُونَ، وَعَلَى رَبِّهِمْ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ

English Translation:

“Seventy thousand people of my followers will enter Paradise without accounts, and they are those who do not practice Ar-Ruqya and do not see an evil omen in things, and put their trust in their Lord.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ gives glad tidings of believers who enter Paradise without reckoning—people who place their trust in their Lord rather than clinging to superstition or fearful omens.

Tawakkul here means relying on Allah with a heart free of anxious reading of signs in every event.

Trust does not erase effort; it frees the heart from false fears.

Reflection

Place your reliance in Allah, not in omens or restless suspicion.

A trusting heart walks lighter toward Him.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6472, Book of To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq).
