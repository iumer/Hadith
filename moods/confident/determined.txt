Quran 3:200

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱصۡبِرُواْ وَصَابِرُواْ وَرَابِطُواْ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

Urdu Translation:

اے ایمان والو! تم ﺛابت قدم رہو اور ایک دوسرے کو تھامے رکھو اور جہاد کے لئے تیار رہو اور اللہ تعالیٰ سے ڈرتے رہو تاکہ تم مراد کو پہنچو

English Translation:

“O you who have believed, persevere and endure and remain stationed and fear Allah that you may be successful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the believers to persevere, endure, remain stationed, and fear Allah so that they may be successful.

Success is tied to long patience, mutual steadfastness, and lasting taqwa.

The path asks for staying power, not only a strong start.

Reflection

Hold your ground with patience and God-consciousness.

Success grows from perseverance that does not quit.

Reference: Quran 3:200, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 41:30

Surah Fussilat (سُورَةُ فُصِّلَتۡ)

Arabic:

إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ

Urdu Translation:

(واقعی) جن لوگوں نے کہا کہ ہمارا پروردگار اللہ ہے پھر اسی پر قائم رہے ان کے پاس فرشتے (یہ کہتے ہوئے آتے ہیں کہ تم کچھ بھی اندیشہ اور غم نہ کرو (بلکہ) اس جنت کی بشارت سن لو جس کا تم وعده دیئے گئے ہو

English Translation:

“Indeed, those who have said, "Our Lord is Allah " and then remained on a right course - the angels will descend upon them, [saying], "Do not fear and do not grieve but receive good tidings of Paradise, which you were promised”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that those who say “Our Lord is Allah” and then remain steadfast will have angels descend upon them, saying not to fear or grieve, and giving glad tidings of Paradise.

Iman followed by istiqamah draws divine comfort at life’s hardest crossings.

Angels become messengers of courage and hope.

Reflection

Say with your life that Allah is your Lord—and stay firm.

Steadfastness invites a comfort that tells you: do not fear, do not grieve.

Reference: Quran 41:30, Surah Fussilat.

Quran 42:43

Surah Ash-Shura (سُورَةُ الشُّورَىٰ)

Arabic:

وَلَمَن صَبَرَ وَغَفَرَ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ

Urdu Translation:

اور جو شخص صبر کر لے اور معاف کردے یقیناً یہ بڑی ہمت کے کاموں میں سے (ایک کام) ہے

English Translation:

“And whoever is patient and forgives - indeed, that is of the matters [requiring] determination”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that whoever is patient and forgives—indeed, that is of the matters requiring determination.

Sabr and pardon are not soft options; they are of the hardest, most resolute deeds.

True strength shows in enduring harm without returning it.

Reflection

Patience and forgiveness take real resolve.

Choose them when it would be easier to strike back.

Reference: Quran 42:43, Surah Ash-Shura.

Quran 46:35

Surah Al-Ahqaf (سُورَةُ الأَحۡقَافِ)

Arabic:

فَٱصۡبِرۡ كَمَا صَبَرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعَزۡمِ مِنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِل لَّهُمۡۚ كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَ مَا يُوعَدُونَ لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا سَاعَةࣰ مِّن نَّهَارِۭۚ بَلَٰغࣱۚ فَهَلۡ يُهۡلَكُ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ

Urdu Translation:

پس (اے پیغمبر!) تم ایسا صبر کرو جیسا صبر عالی ہمت رسولوں نے کیا اور ان کے لئے (عذاب طلب کرنے میں) جلدی نہ کرو، یہ جس دن اس عذاب کو دیکھ لیں گے جس کا وعده دیئے جاتے ہیں تو (یہ معلوم ہونے لگے گا کہ) دن کی ایک گھڑی ہی (دنیا میں) ٹھہرے تھے، یہ ہے پیغام پہنچا دینا، پس بدکاروں کے سوا کوئی ہلاک نہ کیا جائے گا

English Translation:

“So be patient, [O Muhammad], as were those of determination among the messengers and do not be impatient for them. It will be - on the Day they see that which they are promised - as though they had not remained [in the world] except an hour of a day. [This is] notification. And will [any] be destroyed except the defiantly disobedient people”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He instructed the Prophet ﷺ to be patient as were the messengers of determination, and not to be impatient for the outcome.

On the Day they are shown what they were promised, it will seem as if they had not remained except an hour of a day.

Waiting on Allah’s timing is part of prophetic character.

Reflection

Do not rush what Allah has timed with wisdom.

Patience places you in the company of the most determined messengers.

Reference: Quran 46:35, Surah Al-Ahqaf.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6338

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Anas (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِذَا دَعَا أَحَدُكُمْ فَلْيَعْزِمِ الْمَسْأَلَةَ، وَلاَ يَقُولَنَّ اللَّهُمَّ إِنْ شِئْتَ فَأَعْطِنِي‏.‏ فَإِنَّهُ لاَ مُسْتَكْرِهَ لَهُ

Urdu Translation:

جب تم میں سے کوئی دعا کرے تو اللہ سے قطعی طور پر مانگے اور یہ نہ کہے کہ اے اللہ! اگر تو چاہے تو مجھے عطا فرما کیونکہ اللہ پر کوئی زبردستی کرنے والا نہیں ہے

English Translation:

“When anyone of you appeal to Allah for something, he should ask with determination and should not say, 'O Allah, if You wish, give me.', for nobody can force Allah to do something against His Will.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches us to ask Allah with firm resolve, not with hesitant phrasing that weakens the ask—such as “if You wish, give me.”

Nothing can compel Allah against His will; determination in du‘a reflects trust that He hears and responds as He knows best.

Ask with hope, humility, and confidence in His generosity.

Reflection

Bring your needs to Allah with a heart that truly expects His response.

Certainty in asking is part of good manners with your Lord.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6338, Book of Invocations.
