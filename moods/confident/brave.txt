Quran 2:249

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

فَلَمَّا فَصَلَ طَالُوتُ بِٱلۡجُنُودِ قَالَ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُبۡتَلِيكُم بِنَهَرࣲ فَمَن شَرِبَ مِنۡهُ فَلَيۡسَ مِنِّي وَمَن لَّمۡ يَطۡعَمۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ مِنِّيٓ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱغۡتَرَفَ غُرۡفَةَۢ بِيَدِهِۦۚ فَشَرِبُواْ مِنۡهُ إِلَّا قَلِيلࣰ ا مِّنۡهُمۡۚ فَلَمَّا جَاوَزَهُۥ هُوَ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ قَالُواْ لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِجَالُوتَ وَجُنُودِهِۦۚ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَظُنُّونَ أَنَّهُم مُّلَٰقُواْ ٱللَّهِ كَم مِّن فِئَةࣲ قَلِيلَةٍ غَلَبَتۡ فِئَةࣰ كَثِيرَةَۢ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Urdu Translation:

جب (حضرت) طالوت لشکروں کو لے کر نکلے تو کہا سنو اللہ تعالیٰ تمہیں ایک نہر سے آزمانے واﻻہے، جس نے اس میں سے پانی پی لیا وه میرا نہیں اور جو اسے نہ چکھے وه میرا ہے، ہاں یہ اور بات ہے کہ اپنے ہاتھ سے ایک چلو بھرلے۔ لیکن سوائے چند کے باقی سب نے وه پانی پی لیا (حضرت) طالوت مومنین سمیت جب نہر سے گزر گئے تو وه لوگ کہنے لگے آج تو ہم میں طاقت نہیں کہ جالوت اور اس کے لشکروں سے لڑیں۔ لیکن اللہ تعالیٰ کی ملاقات پر یقین رکھنے والوں نے کہا، بسا اوقات چھوٹی اور تھوڑی سی جماعتیں بڑی اور بہت سی جماعتوں پر اللہ کے حکم سے غلبہ پالیتی ہیں، اللہ تعالیٰ صبر والوں کے ساتھ ہے

English Translation:

“And when Saul went forth with the soldiers, he said, "Indeed, Allah will be testing you with a river. So whoever drinks from it is not of me, and whoever does not taste it is indeed of me, excepting one who takes [from it] in the hollow of his hand." But they drank from it, except a [very] few of them. Then when he had crossed it along with those who believed with him, they said, "There is no power for us today against Goliath and his soldiers." But those who were certain that they would meet Allah said, "How many a small company has overcome a large company by permission of Allah. And Allah is with the patient”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah recalls how a small group who trusted Allah said that victory can come through a few over many—by Allah’s permission—and how Allah is with the patient.

Numbers do not decide outcomes; Allah does. Patience and trust can outweigh apparent weakness.

Faith looks past odds to the One who grants success.

Reflection

Do not measure your hope only by how many stand with you.

Allah is with the patient, even when they seem few.

Reference: Quran 2:249, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 3:173

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدۡ جَمَعُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱخۡشَوۡهُمۡ فَزَادَهُمۡ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡوَكِيلُ

Urdu Translation:

وه لوگ کہ جب ان سے لوگوں نے کہا کہ کافروں نے تمہارے مقابلے پر لشکر جمع کر لئے ہیں، تم ان سے خوف کھاؤ تو اس بات نے انہیں ایمان میں اور بڑھا دیا اور کہنے لگے ہمیں اللہ کافی ہے اور وه بہت اچھا کارساز ہے

English Translation:

“Those to whom hypocrites said, "Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them." But it [merely] increased them in faith, and they said, "Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of believers who, when told that people had gathered against them, only increased in faith and said, “Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.”

Threats became fuel for trust rather than collapse.

Hasbunallahu wa ni‘mal wakeel is a refuge in frightening news.

Reflection

When fear-mongering arrives, answer with: Allah is sufficient for us.

Let intimidation increase your trust, not your panic.

Reference: Quran 3:173, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 8:45

Surah Al-Anfaal (سُورَةُ الأَنفَالِ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا لَقِيتُمۡ فِئَةࣰ فَٱثۡبُتُواْ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرࣰ ا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ

Urdu Translation:

اے ایمان والو! جب تم کسی مخالف فوج سے بھڑ جاؤ تو ﺛابت قدم رہو اور بکثرت اللہ کو یاد کرو تاکہ تمہیں کامیابی حاصل ہو

English Translation:

“O you who have believed, when you encounter a company [from the enemy forces], stand firm and remember Allah much that you may be successful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that believers that when they encounter a force, they should stand firm and remember Allah much so that they may be successful.

Steadfastness paired with abundant dhikr is a formula for success under pressure.

Remembrance fuels courage when confrontation comes.

Reflection

In hard encounters, stand firm and fill the moment with remembrance of Allah.

Success is tied to presence with Him under pressure.

Reference: Quran 8:45, Surah Al-Anfaal.

Quran 8:46

Surah Al-Anfaal (سُورَةُ الأَنفَالِ)

Arabic:

وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡۖ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اور اللہ کی اور اس کے رسول کی فرماں برداری کرتے رہو، آپس میں اختلاف نہ کرو ورنہ بزدل ہو جاؤ گے اور تمہاری ہوا اکھڑ جائے گی اور صبر و سہارا رکھو، یقیناً اللہ تعالیٰ صبر کرنے والوں کے ساتھ ہے

English Translation:

“And obey Allah and His Messenger, and do not dispute and [thus] lose courage and [then] your strength would depart; and be patient. Indeed, Allah is with the patient”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that believers to obey Him and His Messenger, not to dispute lest they lose courage and strength, and to be patient—for Allah is with the patient.

Internal conflict drains power; patience restores it.

Obedience and unity protect strength that argument wastes.

Reflection

Avoid disputes that drain courage; choose patience instead.

Allah is with the patient when unity holds.

Reference: Quran 8:46, Surah Al-Anfaal.

Sahih al-Bukhari 2908

Book 56: Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad) (كتاب الجهاد والسير)

Narrated Anas (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَمْ تُرَاعُوا لَمْ تُرَاعُوا

Urdu Translation:

ڈرو مت

English Translation:

“Don't be afraid! Don't be afraid!”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith shows the Prophet ﷺ as the bravest of people. When Medina was alarmed at night, he went ahead toward the sound while others were still gathering.

He returned calming them: “Don’t be afraid,” showing courage that protects others, not just oneself.

Faith can give steadiness when fear spreads through a community.

Reflection

Bravery in faith often looks like moving toward calm for others’ sake.

Ask Allah for a heart that steadies when panic rises.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 2908, Book of Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad).

Sahih Muslim 2307a

Book 43: The Book of Virtues (كتاب الفضائل)

Anas b. Malik reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَمْ تُرَاعُوا لَمْ تُرَاعُوا

Urdu Translation:

خوف میں مبتلا نہ ہو خوف میں مبتلانہ ہو

English Translation:

“There was nothing to be afraid of”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith describes the Prophet ﷺ as the most sublime in character, most generous, and bravest of people.

When Medina was disturbed at night, he went first toward the sound and returned reassuring them that there was nothing to fear.

Courage and gentleness met in him: he faced fear so others could feel safe.

Reflection

Prophetic bravery calms people; it does not shame their fear.

Ask Allah for courage that protects and reassures.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2307a, Book of The Book of Virtues.
