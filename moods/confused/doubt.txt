Quran 2:2

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدࣰ ى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اس کتاب (کے اللہ کی کتاب ہونے) میں کوئی شک نہیں پرہیزگاروں کو راه دکھانے والی ہے

English Translation:

“This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allah”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that this Book—beyond doubt—is guidance for those conscious of Him.

The Quran offers a sure path for hearts that approach with taqwa: reverence, care, and willingness to follow.

Certainty and guidance meet in a Book that does not leave the mindful wandering.

Reflection

Approach the Quran with a heart that wants to be guided.

For the God-conscious, it is a clear and certain light.

Reference: Quran 2:2, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 49:15

Surah Al-Hujuraat (سُورَةُ الحُجُرَاتِ)

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَرۡتَابُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّٰدِقُونَ

Urdu Translation:

مومن تو وه ہیں جو اللہ اور اس کے رسول پر (پکا) ایمان ﻻئیں پھر شک وشبہ نہ کریں اور اپنے مالوں سے اور اپنی جانوں سے اللہ کی راه میں جہاد کرتے رہیں، (اپنے دعوائے ایمان میں) یہی سچے اور راست گو ہیں

English Translation:

“The believers are only the ones who have believed in Allah and His Messenger and then doubt not but strive with their properties and their lives in the cause of Allah. It is those who are the truthful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the believers are those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, then do not doubt, but strive with their wealth and lives in His cause—those are the truthful.

Faith here is conviction joined to sacrifice, not claim alone.

Truthfulness is proven in what we give for Allah.

Reflection

Let belief move you to sincere effort, free of corrosive doubt.

Truth shows in what you are willing to spend for Allah.

Reference: Quran 49:15, Surah Al-Hujuraat.

Sahih al-Bukhari 52

Book 2: Belief (كتاب الإيمان)

Narrated An-Nu'man bin Bashir (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

الْحَلاَلُ بَيِّنٌ وَالْحَرَامُ بَيِّنٌ، وَبَيْنَهُمَا مُشَبَّهَاتٌ لاَ يَعْلَمُهَا كَثِيرٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ، فَمَنِ اتَّقَى الْمُشَبَّهَاتِ اسْتَبْرَأَ لِدِيِنِهِ وَعِرْضِهِ

Urdu Translation:

حلال کھلا ہوا ہے اور حرام بھی کھلا ہوا ہے اور ان دونوں کے درمیان بعض چیزیں شبہ کی ہیں جن کو بہت لوگ نہیں جانتے ( کہ حلال ہیں یا حرام ) پھر جو کوئی شبہ کی چیزوں سے بھی بچ گیا اس نے اپنے دین اور عزت کو بچا لیا

English Translation:

“Both legal and illegal things are evident but in between them there are doubtful (suspicious) things and most of the people have no knowledge about them. So whoever saves himself from these suspicious things saves his religion and his honor”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that lawful and unlawful matters are clear, but between them are doubtful matters most people do not know well.

Whoever avoids these doubtful areas protects religion and honor. Whoever indulges in them risks falling into the forbidden—like a shepherd grazing too near a restricted pasture.

Caution here is wisdom, not rigidity.

Reflection

When your heart feels unsure, stepping back is often an act of faith.

Protecting your religion can begin with avoiding gray areas that pull you toward harm.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 52, Book of Belief.

Sunan Abi Dawud 3330

Book 23: Commercial Transactions (Kitab Al-Buyu) (كتاب البيوع)

Narrated Al-Nu'man b. Bashir (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

وَبَيْنَهُمَا مُشَبَّهَاتٌ لا يَعْلَمُهَا كَثِيرٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ فَمَنِ اتَّقَى الشُّبُهَاتِ اسْتَبْرَأَ عِرْضَهُ وَدِينَهُ وَمَنْ وَقَعَ فِي الشُّبُهَاتِ وَقَعَ فِي الْحَرَامِ

Urdu Translation:

ان دونوں کے درمیان کچھ شبہے کی چیزیں ہیں جنہیں بہت سے لوگ نہیں جانتے، جو شبہوں سے بچا وہ اپنے دین اور اپنی عزت و آبرو کو بچا لے گیا، اور جو شبہوں میں پڑا وہ حرام میں پھنس گیا

English Translation:

“But between them are certain doubtful things which many people do not recognize. He who guards against doubtful things keeps his religion and his honor blameless, but he who falls into doubtful things falls into what is unlawful”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that some matters are clearly lawful, some clearly unlawful, and between them lie doubtful matters many people do not recognize.

Whoever stays away from what is doubtful protects both their religion and their honor. Whoever plunges into doubt is more likely to slip into what is forbidden.

This is a call to carefulness and sincerity, not harshness toward others.

Reflection

When something leaves your heart uneasy, pause and choose the safer path.

Guarding your faith often begins with small, quiet choices.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 3330, Book of Commercial Transactions (Kitab Al-Buyu).
