Quran 1:6

Surah Al-Faatiha (سُورَةُ ٱلْفَاتِحَةِ)

Arabic:

ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ

English Translation:

“Guide us to the straight path”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah is our constant plea: Guide us to the straight path.

We ask not only for knowledge of right, but for a life kept upon it—day after day, choice after choice.

Guidance is a gift we need continually, not a trophy we win once.

Reflection

Keep asking Allah to guide you—especially when you feel unsure.

The straight path is walked with ongoing du‘a.

Reference: Quran 1:6, Surah Al-Faatiha.

Quran 10:57

Surah Yunus (سُورَةُ يُونُسَ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدࣰ ى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

English Translation:

“O mankind, there has to come to you instruction from your Lord and healing for what is in the breasts and guidance and mercy for the believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He has sent instruction from Him—healing for what is in the breasts, guidance, and mercy for the believers.

The Quran is not only information; it is medicine for anxious, wounded, and restless hearts.

Those who receive it with faith find both direction and comfort.

Reflection

When your chest feels tight, turn to the words Allah sent as healing.

Guidance and mercy are waiting in what He revealed.

Reference: Quran 10:57, Surah Yunus.

Quran 17:82

Surah Al-Israa (سُورَةُ الإِسۡرَاءِ)

Arabic:

وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارࣰ ا

English Translation:

“And We send down of the Qur'an that which is healing and mercy for the believers, but it does not increase the wrongdoers except in loss”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He sends down of the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers.

For hearts that receive it with faith, revelation mends and softens. It is a mercy that meets wounds words alone cannot reach.

Approach the Quran as medicine, not only as text.

Reflection

Seek healing in what Allah revealed.

For the believing heart, the Quran is mercy that repairs.

Reference: Quran 17:82, Surah Al-Israa.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3489

Book 48: Chapters on Supplication (كتاب الدعوات عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

It is narrated:

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

English Translation:

“O Allah, indeed, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency (Allāhumma innī as’alukal-hudā wat-tuqā, wal-`afāfa wal-ghinā).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ used to ask Allah for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency.

These four—huda, tuqa, ‘afaf, and ghina—cover direction, God-consciousness, purity, and contentment of need.

A compact dua that shapes a whole life of goodness.

Reflection

Ask Allah for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency.

These gifts steady both the soul and the path.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3489, Book of Chapters on Supplication.
