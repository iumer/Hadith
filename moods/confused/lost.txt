Quran 1:6

Surah Al-Faatiha (سُورَةُ ٱلْفَاتِحَةِ)

Arabic:

ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ

English Translation:

“Guide us to the straight path”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah is our constant plea: Guide us to the straight path.

We ask not only for knowledge of right, but for a life kept upon it—day after day, choice after choice.

Guidance is a gift we need continually, not a trophy we win once.

Reflection

Keep asking Allah to guide you—especially when you feel unsure.

The straight path is walked with ongoing du‘a.

Reference: Quran 1:6, Surah Al-Faatiha.

Quran 93:3

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa (سُورَةُ الضُّحَىٰ)

Arabic:

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ

English Translation:

“Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muhammad], nor has He detested [you]”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the Lord of the Prophet ﷺ has not taken leave of him nor detested him.

In a time when revelation paused and worry could rise, Allah affirmed ongoing care and love.

Silence from expected relief is not abandonment by Allah.

Reflection

If you feel forgotten, remember: your Lord has not left you or turned against you.

Delay is not dislike from the Most Merciful.

Reference: Quran 93:3, Surah Ad-Dhuhaa.

Quran 93:5

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa (سُورَةُ الضُّحَىٰ)

Arabic:

وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ

English Translation:

“And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied.

A future gift from Allah will fill the heart with contentment.

Hope is anchored in His pledge, not in guessing timelines.

Reflection

Trust that Allah will give until your heart is satisfied.

His promise of future fullness can carry you through lean seasons.

Reference: Quran 93:5, Surah Ad-Dhuhaa.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3489

Book 48: Chapters on Supplication (كتاب الدعوات عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

It is narrated:

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

English Translation:

“O Allah, indeed, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency (Allāhumma innī as’alukal-hudā wat-tuqā, wal-`afāfa wal-ghinā).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ used to ask Allah for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency.

These four—huda, tuqa, ‘afaf, and ghina—cover direction, God-consciousness, purity, and contentment of need.

A compact dua that shapes a whole life of goodness.

Reflection

Ask Allah for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency.

These gifts steady both the soul and the path.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3489, Book of Chapters on Supplication.
