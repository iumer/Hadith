Quran 93:6-8

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa (سُورَةُ الضُّحَىٰ)

Arabic:

أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمࣰ ا فَـَٔاوَىٰ ﴿٦﴾ وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلࣰّ ا فَهَدَىٰ ﴿٧﴾ وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلࣰ ا فَأَغۡنَىٰ ﴿٨﴾

Urdu Translation:

(6) کیا اس نے تجھے یتیم پا کر جگہ نہیں دی (7) اور تجھے راه بھوﻻ پا کر ہدایت نہیں دی (8) اور تجھے نادار پاکر تونگر نہیں بنا دیا؟

English Translation:

“(6) Did He not find you an orphan and give [you] refuge (7) And He found you lost and guided [you] (8) And He found you poor and made [you] self-sufficient”

Meaning ❤️

Allah reminds the Prophet ﷺ: did He not find you an orphan and give you shelter, find you lost and guide you, find you in need and make you self-sufficient?

Guidance is something Allah gives to those who feel lost.

Reflection

Feeling lost is not the end of the story.

Ask Him to find you and guide you, as He did before.

Reference: Quran 93:6-8, Surah Ad-Dhuhaa.

Quran 29:69

Surah Al-Ankaboot (سُورَةُ العَنكَبُوتِ)

Arabic:

وَٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ فِينَا لَنَهۡدِيَنَّهُمۡ سُبُلَنَاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اور جو لوگ ہماری راه میں مشقتیں برداشت کرتے ہیں ہم انہیں اپنی راہیں ضرور دکھا دیں گے۔ یقیناً اللہ تعالیٰ نیکو کاروں کا ساتھی ہے

English Translation:

“And those who strive for Us - We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good”

Meaning ❤️

Allah promises those who strive for Him: We will surely guide them to Our paths, and Allah is with those who do good.

The path opens to the one who keeps moving toward Him.

Reflection

You don't need the whole map — just keep striving for Him.

He has promised to show you the way.

Reference: Quran 29:69, Surah Al-Ankaboot.

Sahih Muslim 2577a

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Abu Dharr reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying that Allah, the Exalted and Glorious, said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَا عِبَادِي كُلُّكُمْ ضَالٌّ إِلاَّ مَنْ هَدَيْتُهُ فَاسْتَهْدُونِي أَهْدِكُمْ

Urdu Translation:

میرے بندو! تم سب کے سب گمراہ ہو ، سوائے اس کے جسے میں ہدایت دے دوں ، اس لیے مجھ سے ہدایت مانگو ، میں تمہیں ہدایت دوں گا

English Translation:

“O My servants, all of you are astray except for those I have guided, so seek guidance of Me and I shall guide you”

Meaning ❤️

In this hadith qudsi Allah says: O My servants, all of you are astray except whom I guide — so ask Me for guidance and I will guide you.

Feeling lost is the human condition; guidance is a gift you simply have to ask for.

Reflection

You don't have to find the way alone.

Ask Him directly: O Allah, guide me.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2577a, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.
