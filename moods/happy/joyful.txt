Quran 10:58

Surah Yunus (سُورَةُ يُونُسَ)

Arabic:

قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ

Urdu Translation:

آپ کہہ دیجئے کہ بس لوگوں کو اللہ کے اس انعام اور رحمت پر خوش ہونا چاہئے۔ وه اس سے بدرجہا بہتر ہے جس کو وه جمع کر رہے ہیں

English Translation:

“Say, "In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy - in that let them rejoice; it is better than what they accumulate”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to rejoice in His bounty and mercy—that this joy is better than whatever people accumulate.

True celebration is not only material gain; it is recognizing gifts that come from Allah’s generosity and compassion.

The heart that rejoices in Him finds a deeper gladness than possessions can give.

Reflection

Let your joy be rooted in Allah’s mercy, not only in what you gather.

His bounty is worth more than any collection of things.

Reference: Quran 10:58, Surah Yunus.

Quran 84:9

Surah Al-Inshiqaaq (سُورَةُ الانشِقَاقِ)

Arabic:

وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

اور وه اپنے اہل کی طرف ہنسی خوشی لوٹ آئے گا

English Translation:

“And return to his people in happiness”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of the one who receives the record in the right hand returning to family in happiness.

A joyful homecoming awaits those whose account is made easy.

The end of the story for the successful is gladness, not endless dread.

Reflection

Live for an easy reckoning and a happy return.

Allah describes joy for those who succeed on that Day.

Reference: Quran 84:9, Surah Al-Inshiqaaq.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 766

Book 8: The Book on Fasting (كتاب الصوم عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Abu Hurairah narrated that (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لِلصَّائِمِ فَرْحَتَانِ فَرْحَةٌ حِينَ يُفْطِرُ وَفَرْحَةٌ حِينَ يَلْقَى رَبَّهُ

Urdu Translation:

روزہ دار کے لیے دو خوشیاں ہیں: ایک خوشی افطار کرتے وقت ہوتی ہے اور دوسری اس وقت ہو گی جب وہ اپنے رب سے ملے گا

English Translation:

“There are two joys for the fasting person: the joy when he breaks his fast, and the joy of when he meets his Lord.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that the fasting person has two joys: joy when breaking the fast, and joy when meeting his Lord.

Discipline today holds a taste of relief at sunset and a greater joy in the Hereafter.

Worship carries both immediate and eternal sweetness.

Reflection

Let the joy of iftar remind you of a greater joy still coming.

Fasting links today’s patience to tomorrow’s meeting with Allah.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 766, Book of The Book on Fasting.
