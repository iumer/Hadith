Quran 10:58

Surah Yunus (سُورَةُ يُونُسَ)

Arabic:

قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ

English Translation:

“Say, "In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy - in that let them rejoice; it is better than what they accumulate”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to rejoice in His bounty and mercy—that this joy is better than whatever people accumulate.

True celebration is not only material gain; it is recognizing gifts that come from Allah’s generosity and compassion.

The heart that rejoices in Him finds a deeper gladness than possessions can give.

Reflection

Let your joy be rooted in Allah’s mercy, not only in what you gather.

His bounty is worth more than any collection of things.

Reference: Quran 10:58, Surah Yunus.

Quran 39:17

Surah Az-Zumar (سُورَةُ الزُّمَرِ)

Arabic:

وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَنَبُواْ ٱلطَّٰغُوتَ أَن يَعۡبُدُوهَا وَأَنَابُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰۚ فَبَشِّرۡ عِبَادِ

English Translation:

“But those who have avoided Taghut, lest they worship it, and turned back to Allah - for them are good tidings. So give good tidings to My servants”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that those who avoid false worship and turn back to Him have good tidings—so give good news to His servants.

Leaving what corrupts the heart and returning to Allah is met with glad news, not cold distance.

Repentance is welcomed with hope.

Reflection

Turning back to Allah brings glad tidings.

Leave what pulls you from Him and receive the good news meant for His servants.

Reference: Quran 39:17, Surah Az-Zumar.

Quran 61:13

Surah As-Saff (سُورَةُ الصَّفِّ)

Arabic:

وَأُخۡرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَاۖ نَصۡرࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَتۡحࣱ قَرِيبࣱۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ

English Translation:

“And [you will obtain] another [favor] that you love - victory from Allah and an imminent conquest; and give good tidings to the believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the believers of another favor they love: victory from Allah and an imminent conquest—and to give good tidings to the believers.

Hope is part of the message; glad news is meant to lift hearts that strive.

Allah announces openings before they fully appear.

Reflection

Hold the good tidings Allah gives believers.

Victory from Him can be nearer than your worry suggests.

Reference: Quran 61:13, Surah As-Saff.
