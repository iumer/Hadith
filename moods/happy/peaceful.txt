Quran 13:28

Surah Ar-Ra'd (سُورَةُ الرَّعۡدِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

Urdu Translation:

جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے ان کے دل اللہ کے ذکر سے اطمینان حاصل کرتے ہیں۔ یاد رکھو اللہ کے ذکر سے ہی دلوں کو تسلی حاصل ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that hearts find assurance through His remembrance—and that by the remembrance of Allah, hearts are assured.

Dhikr is not empty repetition; it is the means by which restless hearts settle.

Belief and remembrance together bring a calm the world cannot manufacture.

Reflection

When your heart races, remember Allah until it softens.

True assurance grows in His remembrance.

Reference: Quran 13:28, Surah Ar-Ra'd.

Quran 48:4

Surah Al-Fath (سُورَةُ الفَتۡحِ)

Arabic:

هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡۗ وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

وہی ہے جس نے مسلمانوں کے دلوں میں سکون (اور اطیمنان) ڈال دیا تاکہ اپنے ایمان کے ساتھ ہی ساتھ اور بھی ایمان میں بڑھ جائیں، اور آسمانوں اور زمین کے ﴿کل﴾ لشکر اللہ ہی کے ہیں۔ اور اللہ تعالیٰ دانا باحکمت ہے

English Translation:

“It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their [present] faith. And to Allah belong the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and ever is Allah Knowing and Wise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers so that they would increase in faith upon their faith.

Sakinah is a gift that settles the heart and deepens iman.

Calm from Allah is not emptiness—it is faith growing quieter and stronger.

Reflection

Ask Allah to send tranquillity into your heart.

His sakinah increases faith in ways force never could.

Reference: Quran 48:4, Surah Al-Fath.

Quran 89:27

Surah Al-Fajr (سُورَةُ الفَجۡرِ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ

Urdu Translation:

اے اطمینان والی روح

English Translation:

“[To the righteous it will be said], "O reassured soul”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the reassured soul will be addressed—the nafs that has found peace in Him.

This is the voice of welcome at the journey’s end for a heart settled in faith.

Tranquillity with Allah is a destination worth every struggle toward it.

Reflection

Aspire to become a soul at peace with Allah.

That reassurance is the greeting the righteous long to hear.

Reference: Quran 89:27, Surah Al-Fajr.

Quran 89:28

Surah Al-Fajr (سُورَةُ الفَجۡرِ)

Arabic:

ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةࣰ مَّرۡضِيَّةࣰ‏

Urdu Translation:

تو اپنے رب کی طرف لوٹ چل اس طرح کہ تو اس سے راضی وه تجھ سے خوش

English Translation:

“Return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing [to Him]”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the reassured soul is invited to return to its Lord, well-pleased and pleasing to Him.

The relationship ends in mutual pleasure: the servant content with Allah, and Allah pleased with the servant.

That double ridwan is among the highest hopes of a believer.

Reflection

Live so that your return to Allah is in mutual pleasure.

Seek a heart that is pleased with Him—and pleasing to Him.

Reference: Quran 89:28, Surah Al-Fajr.

Quran 89:29

Surah Al-Fajr (سُورَةُ الفَجۡرِ)

Arabic:

فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي

Urdu Translation:

پس میرے خاص بندوں میں داخل ہو جا

English Translation:

“And enter among My [righteous] servants”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that that soul is invited to enter among His righteous servants.

Belonging to the company of the righteous is part of the final honor.

Faith’s destination includes beautiful fellowship, not isolation.

Reflection

Long to be gathered with Allah’s righteous servants.

Choose companions and habits now that lead toward that company.

Reference: Quran 89:29, Surah Al-Fajr.

Quran 89:30

Surah Al-Fajr (سُورَةُ الفَجۡرِ)

Arabic:

وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي

Urdu Translation:

اور میری جنت میں چلی جا

English Translation:

“And enter My Paradise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us, completing the welcome: enter My Paradise.

After reassurance, return, and joining the righteous comes the entrance into His Jannah.

The path of a settled soul ends in the garden of the Most Merciful.

Reflection

Let Paradise be the aim that softens today’s hardness.

Allah Himself invites the peaceful soul: enter My Paradise.

Reference: Quran 89:30, Surah Al-Fajr.
