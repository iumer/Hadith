Quran 13:28

Surah Ar-Ra'd (سُورَةُ الرَّعۡدِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

Urdu Translation:

جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے ان کے دل اللہ کے ذکر سے اطمینان حاصل کرتے ہیں۔ یاد رکھو اللہ کے ذکر سے ہی دلوں کو تسلی حاصل ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that hearts find assurance through His remembrance—and that by the remembrance of Allah, hearts are assured.

Dhikr is not empty repetition; it is the means by which restless hearts settle.

Belief and remembrance together bring a calm the world cannot manufacture.

Reflection

When your heart races, remember Allah until it softens.

True assurance grows in His remembrance.

Reference: Quran 13:28, Surah Ar-Ra'd.

Quran 48:4

Surah Al-Fath (سُورَةُ الفَتۡحِ)

Arabic:

هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡۗ وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

وہی ہے جس نے مسلمانوں کے دلوں میں سکون (اور اطیمنان) ڈال دیا تاکہ اپنے ایمان کے ساتھ ہی ساتھ اور بھی ایمان میں بڑھ جائیں، اور آسمانوں اور زمین کے ﴿کل﴾ لشکر اللہ ہی کے ہیں۔ اور اللہ تعالیٰ دانا باحکمت ہے

English Translation:

“It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their [present] faith. And to Allah belong the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and ever is Allah Knowing and Wise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers so that they would increase in faith upon their faith.

Sakinah is a gift that settles the heart and deepens iman.

Calm from Allah is not emptiness—it is faith growing quieter and stronger.

Reflection

Ask Allah to send tranquillity into your heart.

His sakinah increases faith in ways force never could.

Reference: Quran 48:4, Surah Al-Fath.

Quran 89:27-30

Surah Al-Fajr (سُورَةُ الفَجۡرِ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ ﴿٢٧﴾ ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةࣰ مَّرۡضِيَّةࣰ‏ ﴿٢٨﴾ فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي ﴿٢٩﴾ وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي ﴿٣٠﴾

Urdu Translation:

(27) اے اطمینان والی روح (28) تو اپنے رب کی طرف لوٹ چل اس طرح کہ تو اس سے راضی وه تجھ سے خوش (29) پس میرے خاص بندوں میں داخل ہو جا (30) اور میری جنت میں چلی جا

English Translation:

“(27) [To the righteous it will be said], "O reassured soul (28) Return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing [to Him] (29) And enter among My [righteous] servants (30) And enter My Paradise”

Meaning ❤️

Allah addresses the soul that found rest in Him: O reassured soul, return to your Lord, pleased and pleasing to Him. Enter among My servants, and enter My Paradise.

The four ayahs together describe the peaceful end of a life lived in trust.

Reflection

Peace with Allah is the peace that lasts.

Live in a way your soul would be glad to be called home from.

Reference: Quran 89:27-30, Surah Al-Fajr.
