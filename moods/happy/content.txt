Quran 93:3-5

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa (سُورَةُ الضُّحَىٰ)

Arabic:

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ ﴿٣﴾ وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ ﴿٤﴾ وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ ﴿٥﴾

Urdu Translation:

(3) نہ تو تیرے رب نے تجھے چھوڑا ہے اور نہ وه بیزار ہو گیا ہے (4) یقیناً تیرے لئے انجام آغاز سے بہتر ہوگا (5) تجھے تیرا رب بہت جلد (انعام) دے گا اور تو راضی (وخوش) ہو جائے گا

English Translation:

“(3) Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muhammad], nor has He detested [you] (4) And the Hereafter is better for you than the first [life] (5) And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied”

Meaning ❤️

Allah reassures the Prophet ﷺ after a pause in revelation: your Lord has not left you, nor is He displeased with you. What is coming is better than what has passed, and He will give until you are satisfied.

Contentment grows from trusting that Allah's giving is not finished.

Reflection

Your Lord has not left you.

What He has prepared is better than what you have now — rest in that.

Reference: Quran 93:3-5, Surah Ad-Dhuhaa.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6446

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) (كتاب الرقاق)

Narrated Abu Huraira (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَيْسَ الْغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ الْعَرَضِ، وَلَكِنَّ الْغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ

Urdu Translation:

تونگری یہ نہیں ہے کہ سامان زیادہ ہو، بلکہ امیری یہ ہے کہ دل غنی ہو

English Translation:

“Wealth is not in having many possessions, but rather (true) wealth is feeling sufficiency in the soul.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that true wealth is not many possessions, but feeling sufficiency in the soul.

A person may own much and still feel empty; another may have little and feel rich because the heart is content.

Contentment is a gift that softens comparison and restless wanting.

Reflection

Ask Allah for richness of the heart, not only increase of things.

Sufficiency in the soul is a quiet form of wealth.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6446, Book of To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq).

Sahih Muslim 1054

Book 12: The Book of Zakat (كتاب الزكاة)

Amr b. al-'As reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَنْ أَسْلَمَ وَرُزِقَ كَفَافًا وَقَنَّعَهُ اللَّهُ بِمَا آتَاهُ

Urdu Translation:

وہ انسان کا میاب و بامراد ہو گیا جو مسلمان ہو گیا اور اسے گزر بسر کے بقدر روزی ملی اور اللہ تعا لیٰ نے اسے جو دیا اس پر قناعت کی تو فیق بخشی

English Translation:

“He Is successful who has accepted Islam, who has been provided with sufficient for his want and been made contented by Allah with what He has given him.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ describes success as accepting Islam, having enough for one’s needs, and being made content by Allah with what He has given.

Success here is not endless increase; it is faith joined with sufficiency and a satisfied heart.

Contentment is itself a form of divine favor.

Reflection

Look for success in faith and contentment, not only in accumulation.

Being pleased with Allah’s gift is part of thriving.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 1054, Book of The Book of Zakat.

Sahih Muslim 2963a

Book 55: The Book of Zuhd and Softening of Hearts  (كتاب الزهد والرقائق)

Abu Huraira reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِذَا نَظَرَ أَحَدُكُمْ إِلَى مَنْ فُضِّلَ عَلَيْهِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْخَلْقِ فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْهُ مِمَّنْ فُضِّلَ عَلَيْهِ

Urdu Translation:

جب تم میں سے کوئی شخص اس کی طرف دیکھے جسے مال اور جمال میں فضیلت حاصل ہے تو اس کو بھی دیکھے جو اس سے کمتر ہے ، جس پر ( خود ) اسے فضیلت حاصل ہے

English Translation:

“When one of you looks at one who stands at a higher level than you in regard to wealth and physical structure he should also see one who stands at a lower level than you in regard to these things (in which he stands) at a higher level (as compared to him).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ gives a simple habit for a content heart: when you notice someone who has more, also notice someone who has less.

Comparing only upward breeds restlessness; looking at the whole picture brings gratitude.

Reflection

Look at what you have been given before you look at what others have.

The blessing you overlook today is someone else's prayer.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2963a, Book of The Book of Zuhd and Softening of Hearts .
