Quran 9:59

Surah At-Tawba (سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ)

Arabic:

وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ رَضُواْ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ سَيُؤۡتِينَا ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ رَٰغِبُونَ

Urdu Translation:

اگر یہ لوگ اللہ اور رسول کے دیئے ہوئے پر خوش رہتے اور کہہ دیتے کہ اللہ ہمیں کافی ہے اللہ ہمیں اپنے فضل سے دے گا اور اس کا رسول بھی، ہم تو اللہ کی ذات سے ہی توقع رکھنے والے ہیں

English Translation:

“If only they had been satisfied with what Allah and His Messenger gave them and said, "Sufficient for us is Allah; Allah will give us of His bounty, and [so will] His Messenger; indeed, we are desirous toward Allah," [it would have been better for them]”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of the better path: being satisfied with what Allah and His Messenger gave, and saying that Allah is sufficient—He will give of His bounty—indeed we are desirous toward Allah.

Contentment and hope in His future giving replace restless complaint.

Desire directed toward Allah is a clean longing.

Reflection

Be content with what Allah has given, and say He is sufficient.

Turn your longing toward His bounty with hope.

Reference: Quran 9:59, Surah At-Tawba.

Quran 93:5

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa (سُورَةُ الضُّحَىٰ)

Arabic:

وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ

Urdu Translation:

تجھے تیرا رب بہت جلد (انعام) دے گا اور تو راضی (وخوش) ہو جائے گا

English Translation:

“And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied.

A future gift from Allah will fill the heart with contentment.

Hope is anchored in His pledge, not in guessing timelines.

Reflection

Trust that Allah will give until your heart is satisfied.

His promise of future fullness can carry you through lean seasons.

Reference: Quran 93:5, Surah Ad-Dhuhaa.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6446

Book 81: To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq) (كتاب الرقاق)

Narrated Abu Huraira (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَيْسَ الْغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ الْعَرَضِ، وَلَكِنَّ الْغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ

Urdu Translation:

تونگری یہ نہیں ہے کہ سامان زیادہ ہو، بلکہ امیری یہ ہے کہ دل غنی ہو

English Translation:

“Wealth is not in having many possessions, but rather (true) wealth is feeling sufficiency in the soul.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that true wealth is not many possessions, but feeling sufficiency in the soul.

A person may own much and still feel empty; another may have little and feel rich because the heart is content.

Contentment is a gift that softens comparison and restless wanting.

Reflection

Ask Allah for richness of the heart, not only increase of things.

Sufficiency in the soul is a quiet form of wealth.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6446, Book of To make the Heart Tender (Ar-Riqaq).

Sahih Muslim 1051

Book 12: The Book of Zakat (كتاب الزكاة)

Abu Huraira reported that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) had said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

لَيْسَ الْغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ الْعَرَضِ وَلَكِنَّ الْغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ

Urdu Translation:

غنا مال و اسباب کی کثرت سے نہیں بلکہ حقیقی غنا دل کی بے نیا زی ہے

English Translation:

“Richness does not lie in the abundance of (worldly) goods but richness is the richness of the soul (heart, self).”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ teaches that richness does not lie in abundant worldly goods, but in richness of the soul.

Outer plenty can coexist with inner poverty. Inner contentment can make modest means feel complete.

This hadith redirects our measure of wealth from what we hold to how our heart rests.

Reflection

Pray for a heart that feels enough.

True richness begins where comparison ends.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 1051, Book of The Book of Zakat.

Sahih Muslim 1054

Book 12: The Book of Zakat (كتاب الزكاة)

Amr b. al-'As reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَنْ أَسْلَمَ وَرُزِقَ كَفَافًا وَقَنَّعَهُ اللَّهُ بِمَا آتَاهُ

Urdu Translation:

وہ انسان کا میاب و بامراد ہو گیا جو مسلمان ہو گیا اور اسے گزر بسر کے بقدر روزی ملی اور اللہ تعا لیٰ نے اسے جو دیا اس پر قناعت کی تو فیق بخشی

English Translation:

“He Is successful who has accepted Islam, who has been provided with sufficient for his want and been made contented by Allah with what He has given him.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ describes success as accepting Islam, having enough for one’s needs, and being made content by Allah with what He has given.

Success here is not endless increase; it is faith joined with sufficiency and a satisfied heart.

Contentment is itself a form of divine favor.

Reflection

Look for success in faith and contentment, not only in accumulation.

Being pleased with Allah’s gift is part of thriving.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 1054, Book of The Book of Zakat.
