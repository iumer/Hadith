Quran 2:152

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ

Urdu Translation:

اس لئے تم میرا ذکر کرو، میں بھی تمہیں یاد کروں گا، میری شکر گزاری کرو اور ناشکری سے بچو

English Translation:

“So remember Me; I will remember you. And be grateful to Me and do not deny Me”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us: remember Me; I will remember you—and be grateful to Me and do not deny Me.

Remembrance is a mutual nearness: our dhikr is met by His care. Gratitude keeps the heart from covering over clear blessings.

A remembered Lord remembers His servant.

Reflection

Make space to remember Allah, and know that He remembers you.

Gratitude keeps the heart from forgetting who provides.

Reference: Quran 2:152, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 14:7

Surah Ibrahim (سُورَةُ إِبۡرَاهِيمَ)

Arabic:

وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ‏

Urdu Translation:

اور جب تمہارے پروردگار نے تمہیں آگاه کر دیا کہ اگر تم شکر گزاری کرو گے تو بیشک میں تمہیں زیاده دوں گا اور اگر تم ناشکری کرو گے تو یقیناً میرا عذاب بہت سخت ہے

English Translation:

“And [remember] when your Lord proclaimed, 'If you are grateful, I will surely increase you [in favor]; but if you deny, indeed, My punishment is severe”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that if we are grateful, He will surely increase us; and if we deny His favors, His punishment is severe.

Gratitude opens the door to more—more peace, more awareness, more blessing in what we already have.

Shukr is both thanks and a way of living awake to gifts.

Reflection

Practice gratitude, and watch how Allah expands what you have.

Thankfulness invites increase from the Most Generous.

Reference: Quran 14:7, Surah Ibrahim.

Quran 16:18

Surah An-Nahl (سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ)

Arabic:

وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ‏

Urdu Translation:

اور اگر تم اللہ کی نعمتوں کا شمار کرنا چاہو تو تم اسے نہیں کر سکتے۔ بیشک اللہ بڑا بخشنے واﻻ مہربان ہے

English Translation:

“And if you should count the favors of Allah, you could not enumerate them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that if we tried to count His favors, we could not enumerate them—and that He is Forgiving and Merciful.

Blessings surround us beyond what we notice: breath, guidance, second chances, unseen protection.

Awareness of countless favors softens the heart toward gratitude and hope.

Reflection

You are living inside more gifts than you can count.

Let that overwhelm turn into quiet thanks to the Forgiving, the Merciful.

Reference: Quran 16:18, Surah An-Nahl.

Sahih Muslim 2734a

Book 48: The Book Pertaining to the Remembrance of Allah, Supplication, Repentance and Seeking Forgiveness  (كتاب الذكر والدعاء والتوبة والاستغفار)

Anas b. Malik reported that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَيَرْضَى عَنِ الْعَبْدِ أَنْ يَأْكُلَ الأَكْلَةَ فَيَحْمَدَهُ عَلَيْهَا أَوْ يَشْرَبَ الشَّرْبَةَ فَيَحْمَدَهُ عَلَيْهَا

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ اس بات پر ( اپنے ) بندے سے راضی ہوتا ہے کہ وہ ایک کھانا کھائے اور اس پر اللہ کی حمد کرے یا پینے کی کوئی چیز ( پانی ، دودھ وغیرہ ) پیے اور اس پر اللہ کی حمد کرے

English Translation:

“Allah is pleased with His servant who says: Al-Hamdu lillah while taking a morsel of food and while drinking.”

Meaning ❤️

Saying Alhamdulillah over a single meal or drink earns Allah's pleasure.

The smallest blessings, noticed and thanked, become a door to His love.

Reflection

Thank Him for the ordinary things today — water, food, breath.

A grateful heart is built one Alhamdulillah at a time.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2734a, Book of The Book Pertaining to the Remembrance of Allah, Supplication, Repentance and Seeking Forgiveness .
