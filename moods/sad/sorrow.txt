Quran 12:86

Surah Yusuf (سُورَةُ يُوسُفَ)

Arabic:

قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Urdu Translation:

انہوں نے کہا کہ میں تو اپنی پریشانیوں اور رنج کی فریاد اللہ ہی سے کر رہا ہوں، مجھے اللہ کی طرف سے وه باتیں معلوم ہیں جو تم نہیں جانتے

English Translation:

“He said, "I only complain of my suffering and my grief to Allah, and I know from Allah that which you do not know”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah captures Ya‘qub’s words: he complains of his suffering and grief only to Allah, knowing from Allah what others do not know.

Pouring pain out to Allah is not complaining against Him; it is seeking Him as the One who understands.

Private du‘a can hold what the world cannot carry.

Reflection

Take your sorrow to Allah before you take it everywhere else.

He knows what others cannot see in your story.

Reference: Quran 12:86, Surah Yusuf.

Quran 16:127

Surah An-Nahl (سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ)

Arabic:

وَٱصۡبِرۡ وَمَا صَبۡرُكَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا تَكُ فِي ضَيۡقࣲ مِّمَّا يَمۡكُرُونَ

Urdu Translation:

آپ صبر کریں بغیر توفیق الٰہی کے آپ صبر کر ہی نہیں سکتے اور ان کے حال پر رنجیده نہ ہوں اور جو مکر وفریب یہ کرتے رہتے ہیں ان سے تنگ دل نہ ہوں

English Translation:

“And be patient, [O Muhammad], and your patience is not but through Allah. And do not grieve over them and do not be in distress over what they conspire”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He instructed the Prophet ﷺ to be patient—and that patience comes only through Allah—and not to grieve over those who turn away or feel distress because of their plots.

Sabr is sustained by Allah’s help, not by sheer willpower alone.

We are invited to release what we cannot control into His care.

Reflection

Ask Allah for the patience you cannot produce alone.

Do not let others’ rejection consume the peace He wants for your heart.

Reference: Quran 16:127, Surah An-Nahl.

Quran 57:23

Surah Al-Hadid (سُورَةُ الحَدِيدِ)

Arabic:

لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالࣲ فَخُورٍ

Urdu Translation:

تاکہ تم اپنے سے فوت شده کسی چیز پر رنجیده نہ ہو جایا کرو اور نہ عطا کرده چیز پر اترا جاؤ، اور اترانے والے شیخی خوروں کو اللہ پسند نہیں فرماتا

English Translation:

“In order that you not despair over what has eluded you and not exult [in pride] over what He has given you. And Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that decree is so you do not despair over what eludes you and do not exult in pride over what He has given—and He does not like the self-deluded and boastful.

Neither crushing regret nor arrogant gloating fits a heart that knows all is from Allah.

Balance comes from seeing His hand in both gain and loss.

Reflection

Do not drown in what you missed or boast in what you received.

Both were from Allah—meet them with humility.

Reference: Quran 57:23, Surah Al-Hadid.

Sahih Muslim 2573

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Abu Sa'id and abu Huraira reported that they heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

مَا يُصِيبُ الْمُؤْمِنَ مِنْ وَصَبٍ وَلاَ نَصَبٍ وَلاَ سَقَمٍ وَلاَ حَزَنٍ حَتَّى الْهَمِّ يُهَمُّهُ إِلاَّ كُفِّرَ بِهِ مِنْ سَيِّئَاتِهِ

Urdu Translation:

مومن کو کوئی تکلیف نہیں پہنچتی ، نہ تھکاوٹ ، نہ بیماری ، نہ غم حتی کہ کوئی فکر بھی جو اسے فکرمند کر دے مگر اس کے بدلے اس کے گناہوں کو مٹا دیا جاتا ہے

English Translation:

“Never a believer is stricken with discomfort, hardship or illness, grief or even with mental worry that his sins are not expiated for him”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith reminds us that no pain or suffering a believer experiences goes unnoticed by Allah.

Whether you’re experiencing physical pain, emotional distress, sadness, anxiety, or exhaustion, Allah can make these hardships a means of forgiveness.

Even something as small as being pricked by a thorn can become a reason for sins to be erased.

Reflection

Your suffering is never meaningless in the sight of Allah.

Sometimes, the difficulties we experience become a means through which Allah purifies us of our sins.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2573, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6369

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

Urdu Translation:

اے اللہ! میں تیری پناہ مانگتا ہوں غم و الم سے، عاجزی سے، سستی سے، بزدلی سے، بخل، قرض چڑھ جانے اور لوگوں کے غلبہ سے

English Translation:

“O Allah! I seek refuge with You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from being heavily in debt and from being overpowered by (other) men.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge in Allah from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from crushing debt and being overpowered by people.

This dua shows that emotional burdens, spiritual stagnation, and worldly pressures are all things we may bring to Allah.

Even the Messenger ﷺ modeled asking for protection from what weighs on the heart.

Reflection

You are allowed to name your fears before Allah.

Seek His refuge from worry, grief, and whatever leaves you feeling small or stuck.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369, Book of Invocations.
