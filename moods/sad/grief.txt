Quran 2:155

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُم بِشَيۡءࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ وَٱلۡجُوعِ وَنَقۡصࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَنفُسِ وَٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اور ہم کسی نہ کسی طرح تمہاری آزمائش ضرور کریں گے، دشمن کے ڈر سے، بھوک پیاس سے، مال وجان اور پھلوں کی کمی سے اور ان صبر کرنے والوں کو خوشخبری دے دیجیئے

English Translation:

“And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that we will surely be tested with fear, hunger, and loss of wealth, lives, and fruits—yet He gives good news to the patient.

Trials are part of the path, not proof of abandonment.

Glad tidings belong to those who meet testing with sabr.

Reflection

Hardship does not mean Allah has left you—it may be the test He told you would come.

Patience carries a promise of good news.

Reference: Quran 2:155, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 2:156

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَٰبَتۡهُم مُّصِيبَةࣱ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّآ إِلَيۡهِ رَٰجِعُونَ

Urdu Translation:

جنہیں، جب کبھی کوئی مصیبت آتی ہے تو کہہ دیا کرتے ہیں کہ ہم تو خود اللہ تعالیٰ کی ملکیت ہیں اور ہم اسی کی طرف لوٹنے والے ہیں

English Translation:

“Who, when disaster strikes them, say, "Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of those who, when disaster strikes, say: “Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return.”

This statement recenters the heart: we are His, and our journey ends with Him.

Belonging to Allah gives meaning to loss and direction to grief.

Reflection

In calamity, return your heart to: we are Allah’s, and to Him we return.

That truth can steady you when everything else shakes.

Reference: Quran 2:156, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 2:157

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَلَوَٰتࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَرَحۡمَةࣱۖ وَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدُونَ

Urdu Translation:

ان پر ان کے رب کی نوازشیں اور رحمتیں ہیں اور یہی لوگ ہدایت یافتہ ہیں

English Translation:

“Those are the ones upon whom are blessings from their Lord and mercy. And it is those who are the [rightly] guided”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that those who meet disaster with remembrance of belonging to Him receive blessings and mercy from their Lord—and they are the rightly guided.

Patience joined with that confession draws down salawat and rahmah.

Guidance is visible in how we respond when life breaks open.

Reflection

Blessings and mercy meet the patient heart that turns to Allah in loss.

That response is itself a sign of being guided.

Reference: Quran 2:157, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 3:139

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Urdu Translation:

تم نہ سستی کرو اور غمگین ہو، تم ہی غالب رہو گے، اگر تم ایماندار ہو

English Translation:

“So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are [true] believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us not to weaken and not to grieve—for you will be superior if you are true believers.

Low spirits after setbacks are met with a call to rise again in faith.

Belief itself becomes a source of dignity and strength.

Reflection

Do not let grief convince you that you are finished.

True faith lifts the heart above despair.

Reference: Quran 3:139, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 12:84

Surah Yusuf (سُورَةُ يُوسُفَ)

Arabic:

وَتَوَلَّىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَسَفَىٰ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ وَٱبۡيَضَّتۡ عَيۡنَاهُ مِنَ ٱلۡحُزۡنِ فَهُوَ كَظِيمࣱ‏

Urdu Translation:

پھر ان سے منھ پھیر لیا اور کہا ہائے یوسف! ان کی آنکھیں بوجہ رنج وغم کے سفید ہو چکی تھیں اور وه غم کو دبائے ہوئے تھے

English Translation:

“And he turned away from them and said, "Oh, my sorrow over Joseph," and his eyes became white from grief, for he was [of that] a suppressor”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah shows Prophet Ya‘qub turning away in sorrow over Yusuf, his eyes whitened from grief, yet he remained a master of suppressed emotion—holding pain without collapsing into despair of Allah.

Even prophets felt deep human loss.

Grief can be intense and still sit within a life of faith.

Reflection

Deep sorrow does not mean you have left Allah.

You may ache greatly and still keep your hope turned toward Him.

Reference: Quran 12:84, Surah Yusuf.

Quran 12:86

Surah Yusuf (سُورَةُ يُوسُفَ)

Arabic:

قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Urdu Translation:

انہوں نے کہا کہ میں تو اپنی پریشانیوں اور رنج کی فریاد اللہ ہی سے کر رہا ہوں، مجھے اللہ کی طرف سے وه باتیں معلوم ہیں جو تم نہیں جانتے

English Translation:

“He said, "I only complain of my suffering and my grief to Allah, and I know from Allah that which you do not know”

Meaning ❤️

This ayah captures Ya‘qub’s words: he complains of his suffering and grief only to Allah, knowing from Allah what others do not know.

Pouring pain out to Allah is not complaining against Him; it is seeking Him as the One who understands.

Private du‘a can hold what the world cannot carry.

Reflection

Take your sorrow to Allah before you take it everywhere else.

He knows what others cannot see in your story.

Reference: Quran 12:86, Surah Yusuf.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1283

Book 23: Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz) (كتاب الجنائز)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنَّمَا الصَّبْرُ عِنْدَ الصَّدْمَةِ الأُولَى

Urdu Translation:

صبر تو جب صدمہ شروع ہو اس وقت کرنا چاہیے

English Translation:

“Verily, the patience is at the first stroke of a calamity”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith shows a grieving woman by a grave who was told to be patient, yet she did not recognize the Prophet ﷺ and pushed him away in her pain.

When she later learned who he was, he taught her that real patience is at the first strike of calamity—when the shock is freshest.

Grief is human; the call is to hold onto Allah even in the first wave of hurt.

Reflection

Patience is hardest—and most rewarded—at the beginning of loss.

Even when pain clouds recognition, returning to Allah remains open.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 1283, Book of Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz).

Sahih al-Bukhari 1303

Book 23: Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz) (كتاب الجنائز)

Narrated Anas bin Malik (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

إِنَّ الْعَيْنَ تَدْمَعُ، وَالْقَلْبَ يَحْزَنُ، وَلاَ نَقُولُ إِلاَّ مَا يَرْضَى رَبُّنَا، وَإِنَّا بِفِرَاقِكَ يَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ لَمَحْزُونُونَ

Urdu Translation:

آنکھوں سے آنسو جاری ہیں اور دل غم سے نڈھال ہے پر زبان سے ہم کہیں گے وہی جو ہمارے پروردگار کو پسند ہے اور اے ابراہیم! ہم تمہاری جدائی سے غمگین ہیں

English Translation:

“The eyes are shedding tears and the heart is grieved, and we will not say except what pleases our Lord, O Ibrahim ! Indeed we are grieved by your separation”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith shows the Prophet ﷺ weeping at the death of his infant son Ibrahim.

When asked about his tears, he said they were mercy. His eyes shed tears and his heart grieved, yet he would only say what pleased his Lord.

Islam does not ask us to freeze our feelings; it teaches us to grieve with dignity and trust.

Reflection

Tears are not a lack of faith—they can be mercy.

You may feel deep sadness and still keep your tongue soft with Allah.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 1303, Book of Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz).

Sahih Muslim 2573

Book 45: The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship (كتاب البر والصلة والآداب)

Abu Sa'id and abu Huraira reported that they heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

مَا يُصِيبُ الْمُؤْمِنَ مِنْ وَصَبٍ وَلاَ نَصَبٍ وَلاَ سَقَمٍ وَلاَ حَزَنٍ حَتَّى الْهَمِّ يُهَمُّهُ إِلاَّ كُفِّرَ بِهِ مِنْ سَيِّئَاتِهِ

Urdu Translation:

مومن کو کوئی تکلیف نہیں پہنچتی ، نہ تھکاوٹ ، نہ بیماری ، نہ غم حتی کہ کوئی فکر بھی جو اسے فکرمند کر دے مگر اس کے بدلے اس کے گناہوں کو مٹا دیا جاتا ہے

English Translation:

“Never a believer is stricken with discomfort, hardship or illness, grief or even with mental worry that his sins are not expiated for him”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith reminds us that no pain or suffering a believer experiences goes unnoticed by Allah.

Whether you’re experiencing physical pain, emotional distress, sadness, anxiety, or exhaustion, Allah can make these hardships a means of forgiveness.

Even something as small as being pricked by a thorn can become a reason for sins to be erased.

Reflection

Your suffering is never meaningless in the sight of Allah.

Sometimes, the difficulties we experience become a means through which Allah purifies us of our sins.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2573, Book of The Book of Virtue, Enjoining Good Manners, and Joining of the Ties of Kinship.

Sahih Muslim 923a

Book 11: The Book of Prayer - Funerals (كتاب الجنائز)

Usama b. Zaid reported (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

ارْجِعْ إِلَيْهَا فَأَخْبِرْهَا إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا أَخَذَ وَلَهُ مَا أَعْطَى وَكُلُّ شَىْءٍ عِنْدَهُ بِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى فَمُرْهَا فَلْتَصْبِرْ وَلْتَحْتَسِبْ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ ہی کا ہے جو اس نے لے لیا اوراسی کا ہے ۔ جو اس نے دیا تھا اور اس کے ہاں ہر چیز کا وقت مقرر ہے ، اور ان کو بتادو کہ وہ صبر کریں اور اجر وثواب کی طلب گار ہوں

English Translation:

“What Allah had taken belonged to Him, and to him belonged what He granted; and He has an appointed time for everything. So you (the messenger) order her to show endurance and seek reward from Allah”

Meaning ❤️

This hadith shows the Prophet ﷺ weeping as a child’s soul departed, his eyes welling with tears of mercy.

When companions were surprised, he affirmed that this was compassion—not a lack of trust in Allah.

The most complete faith can still feel the ache of loss.

Reflection

Mercy softens the eyes without weakening the heart’s trust.

You may weep and still belong fully to Allah.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 923a, Book of The Book of Prayer - Funerals.
