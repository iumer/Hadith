Quran 2:216

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ وَهُوَ كُرۡهࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَهُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تُحِبُّواْ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَهُوَ شَرࣱّ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Urdu Translation:

تم پر جہاد فرض کیا گیا گو وه تمہیں دشوار معلوم ہو، ممکن ہے کہ تم کسی چیز کو بری جانو اور دراصل وہی تمہارے لئے بھلی ہو اور یہ بھی ممکن ہے کہ تم کسی چیز کو اچھی سمجھو، حاﻻنکہ وه تمہارے لئے بری ہو، حقیقی علم اللہ ہی کو ہے، تم محض بےخبر ہو

English Translation:

“Fighting has been enjoined upon you while it is hateful to you. But perhaps you hate a thing and it is good for you; and perhaps you love a thing and it is bad for you. And Allah Knows, while you know not”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that we may hate a thing while it is good for us, and love a thing while it is bad for us—and Allah knows while we do not know.

Our preferences are limited; His wisdom is complete.

What feels bitter may hold benefit we cannot yet see.

Reflection

Trust that Allah’s knowledge outruns your likes and dislikes.

What you resist today may be mercy unfolding slowly.

Reference: Quran 2:216, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 3:139

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

Urdu Translation:

تم نہ سستی کرو اور غمگین ہو، تم ہی غالب رہو گے، اگر تم ایماندار ہو

English Translation:

“So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are [true] believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us not to weaken and not to grieve—for you will be superior if you are true believers.

Low spirits after setbacks are met with a call to rise again in faith.

Belief itself becomes a source of dignity and strength.

Reflection

Do not let grief convince you that you are finished.

True faith lifts the heart above despair.

Reference: Quran 3:139, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 57:23

Surah Al-Hadid (سُورَةُ الحَدِيدِ)

Arabic:

لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالࣲ فَخُورٍ

Urdu Translation:

تاکہ تم اپنے سے فوت شده کسی چیز پر رنجیده نہ ہو جایا کرو اور نہ عطا کرده چیز پر اترا جاؤ، اور اترانے والے شیخی خوروں کو اللہ پسند نہیں فرماتا

English Translation:

“In order that you not despair over what has eluded you and not exult [in pride] over what He has given you. And Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that decree is so you do not despair over what eludes you and do not exult in pride over what He has given—and He does not like the self-deluded and boastful.

Neither crushing regret nor arrogant gloating fits a heart that knows all is from Allah.

Balance comes from seeing His hand in both gain and loss.

Reflection

Do not drown in what you missed or boast in what you received.

Both were from Allah—meet them with humility.

Reference: Quran 57:23, Surah Al-Hadid.

Sahih Muslim 2999

Book 55: The Book of Zuhd and Softening of Hearts  (كتاب الزهد والرقائق)

Suhaib reported that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

عَجَبًا لأَمْرِ الْمُؤْمِنِ إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ وَلَيْسَ ذَاكَ لأَحَدٍ إِلاَّ لِلْمُؤْمِنِ إِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ سَرَّاءُ شَكَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ ضَرَّاءُ صَبَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ

Urdu Translation:

مومن کا معاملہ عجیب ہے ۔ اس کا ہرمعاملہ اس کے لیے بھلائی کا ہے ۔ اور یہ بات مومن کے سوا کسی اور کومیسر نہیں ۔ اسے خوشی اور خوشحالی ملے توشکر کرتا ہے ۔ اور یہ اس کے لیے اچھا ہوتا ہے اور اگر اسے کوئی نقصان پہنچے تو ( اللہ کی رضا کے لیے ) صبر کر تا ہے ، یہ ( ابھی ) اس کے لیے بھلائی ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Strange are the ways of a believer for there is good in every affair of his and this is not the case with anyone else except in the case of a believer for if he has an occasion to feel delight, he thanks (God), thus there is a good for him in it, and if he gets into trouble and shows resignation (and endures it patiently), there is a good for him in it.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ marvels at the believer’s affair: there is good for him in every situation.

If something delightful comes, he thanks Allah—and that is good for him. If hardship comes and he shows patience—that too is good for him.

This beautiful outlook belongs uniquely to the believer who meets life with shukr and sabr.

Reflection

In ease, thank Him; in hardship, be patient—both are paths of good.

Nothing that reaches a believing heart has to be empty of meaning.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2999, Book of The Book of Zuhd and Softening of Hearts .
