Quran 13:28

Surah Ar-Ra'd (سُورَةُ الرَّعۡدِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

Urdu Translation:

جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے ان کے دل اللہ کے ذکر سے اطمینان حاصل کرتے ہیں۔ یاد رکھو اللہ کے ذکر سے ہی دلوں کو تسلی حاصل ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that hearts find assurance through His remembrance—and that by the remembrance of Allah, hearts are assured.

Dhikr is not empty repetition; it is the means by which restless hearts settle.

Belief and remembrance together bring a calm the world cannot manufacture.

Reflection

When your heart races, remember Allah until it softens.

True assurance grows in His remembrance.

Reference: Quran 13:28, Surah Ar-Ra'd.

Quran 39:22

Surah Az-Zumar (سُورَةُ الزُّمَرِ)

Arabic:

أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورࣲ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينٍ

Urdu Translation:

کیا وه شخص جس کا سینہ اللہ تعالیٰ نےاسلام کے لئے کھول دیا ہے پس وه اپنے پروردگار کی طرف سے ایک نور پر ہے اور ہلاکی ہے ان پر جن کے دل یاد الٰہی سے (اﺛر نہیں لیتے بلکہ) سخت ہو گئے ہیں۔ یہ لوگ صریح گمراہی میں (مبتلا) ہیں

English Translation:

“So is one whose breast Allah has expanded to [accept] Islam and he is upon a light from his Lord [like one whose heart rejects it]? Then woe to those whose hearts are hardened against the remembrance of Allah. Those are in manifest error”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to consider whether one whose breast He has expanded to Islam—walking in light from his Lord—is like one whose heart is hardened against Allah’s remembrance.

An opened chest and inner light are gifts that make faith feel spacious.

Hardness toward dhikr is a loss; expansion toward Islam is mercy.

Reflection

Ask Allah to expand your chest with Islam and fill it with His light.

A soft, remembering heart is a treasure from Him.

Reference: Quran 39:22, Surah Az-Zumar.
