Quran 13:28

Surah Ar-Ra'd (سُورَةُ الرَّعۡدِ)

Arabic:

ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ

Urdu Translation:

جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے ان کے دل اللہ کے ذکر سے اطمینان حاصل کرتے ہیں۔ یاد رکھو اللہ کے ذکر سے ہی دلوں کو تسلی حاصل ہوتی ہے

English Translation:

“Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that hearts find assurance through His remembrance—and that by the remembrance of Allah, hearts are assured.

Dhikr is not empty repetition; it is the means by which restless hearts settle.

Belief and remembrance together bring a calm the world cannot manufacture.

Reflection

When your heart races, remember Allah until it softens.

True assurance grows in His remembrance.

Reference: Quran 13:28, Surah Ar-Ra'd.

Quran 39:22

Surah Az-Zumar (سُورَةُ الزُّمَرِ)

Arabic:

أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورࣲ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينٍ

Urdu Translation:

کیا وه شخص جس کا سینہ اللہ تعالیٰ نےاسلام کے لئے کھول دیا ہے پس وه اپنے پروردگار کی طرف سے ایک نور پر ہے اور ہلاکی ہے ان پر جن کے دل یاد الٰہی سے (اﺛر نہیں لیتے بلکہ) سخت ہو گئے ہیں۔ یہ لوگ صریح گمراہی میں (مبتلا) ہیں

English Translation:

“So is one whose breast Allah has expanded to [accept] Islam and he is upon a light from his Lord [like one whose heart rejects it]? Then woe to those whose hearts are hardened against the remembrance of Allah. Those are in manifest error”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us to consider whether one whose breast He has expanded to Islam—walking in light from his Lord—is like one whose heart is hardened against Allah’s remembrance.

An opened chest and inner light are gifts that make faith feel spacious.

Hardness toward dhikr is a loss; expansion toward Islam is mercy.

Reflection

Ask Allah to expand your chest with Islam and fill it with His light.

A soft, remembering heart is a treasure from Him.

Reference: Quran 39:22, Surah Az-Zumar.

Quran 16:97

Surah An-Nahl (سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ)

Arabic:

مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحࣰ ا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةࣰ طَيِّبَةࣰۖ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ

Urdu Translation:

جو شخص نیک عمل کرے مرد ہو یا عورت، لیکن باایمان ہو تو ہم اسے یقیناً نہایت بہتر زندگی عطا فرمائیں گے۔ اور ان کے نیک اعمال کا بہتر بدلہ بھی انہیں ضرور ضرور دیں گے

English Translation:

“Whoever does righteousness, whether male or female, while he is a believer - We will surely cause him to live a good life, and We will surely give them their reward [in the Hereafter] according to the best of what they used to do”

Meaning ❤️

Allah promises a good life (hayatan tayyibah) to every believer, man or woman, who does good — and reward according to the best of what they did.

Fullness of life is promised through faith and good deeds, not possessions.

Reflection

Do one good thing today with faith in your heart.

This is how Allah fills an empty life with meaning.

Reference: Quran 16:97, Surah An-Nahl.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6407

Book 80: Invocations (كتاب الدعوات)

Narrated Abu Musa (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

مَثَلُ الَّذِي يَذْكُرُ رَبَّهُ وَالَّذِي لاَ يَذْكُرُ مَثَلُ الْحَىِّ وَالْمَيِّتِ

Urdu Translation:

اس شخص کی مثال جو اپنے رب کو یاد کرتا ہے اور اس کی مثال جو اپنے رب کو یاد نہیں کرتا زندہ اور مردہ جیسی ہے

English Translation:

“The example of the one who celebrates the Praises of his Lord (Allah) in comparison to the one who does not celebrate the Praises of his Lord, is that of a living creature compared to a dead one.”

Meaning ❤️

The Prophet ﷺ compares the one who remembers his Lord and the one who doesn't to the living and the dead.

The feeling of emptiness can be a sign that the heart is thirsty for dhikr.

Reflection

Begin with one small remembrance: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar.

Let your heart come back to life slowly.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6407, Book of Invocations.
