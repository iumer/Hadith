Quran 2:286

Surah Al-Baqara (سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ)

Arabic:

لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرࣰ ا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اللہ تعالیٰ کسی جان کو اس کی طاقت سے زیاده تکلیف نہیں دیتا، جو نیکی وه کرے وه اس کے لئے اور جو برائی وه کرے وه اس پر ہے، اے ہمارے رب! اگر ہم بھول گئے ہوں یا خطا کی ہو تو ہمیں نہ پکڑنا، اے ہمارے رب! ہم پر وه بوجھ نہ ڈال جو ہم سے پہلے لوگوں پر ڈاﻻ تھا، اے ہمارے رب! ہم پر وه بوجھ نہ ڈال جس کی ہمیں طاقت نہ ہو اور ہم سے درگزر فرما! اور ہمیں بخش دے اور ہم پر رحم کر! تو ہی ہمارا مالک ہے، ہمیں کافروں کی قوم پر غلبہ عطا فرما

English Translation:

“Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity. It will have [the consequence of] what [good] it has gained, and it will bear [the consequence of] what [evil] it has earned. "Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we have forgotten or erred. Our Lord, and lay not upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord, and burden us not with that which we have no ability to bear. And pardon us; and forgive us; and have mercy upon us. You are our protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that He does not charge a soul except with what is within its capacity.

Each person will have the consequence of what good they earn and what they do of harm—and the believers pray for ease, forgiveness, and help against the disbelieving people.

You are not asked to carry what Allah knows you cannot bear.

Reflection

The burden on you was measured by the One who made you.

Ask Him for ease, pardon, and help—He knows your limits.

Reference: Quran 2:286, Surah Al-Baqara.

Quran 65:2

Surah At-Talaaq (سُورَةُ الطَّلَاقِ)

Arabic:

فَإِذَا بَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَأَمۡسِكُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ فَارِقُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفࣲ وَأَشۡهِدُواْ ذَوَيۡ عَدۡلࣲ مِّنكُمۡ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلشَّهَٰدَةَ لِلَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ يُوعَظُ بِهِۦ مَن كَانَ يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

پس جب یہ عورتیں اپنی عدت پوری کرنے کے قریب پہنچ جائیں تو انہیں یا تو قاعده کے مطابق اپنے نکاح میں رہنے دو یا دستور کے مطابق انہیں الگ کر دو اور آپس میں سے دو عادل شخصوں کو گواه کر لو اور اللہ کی رضامندی کے لیے ٹھیک ٹھیک گواہی دو۔ یہی ہے وه جس کی نصیحت اسے کی جاتی ہے جو اللہ پر اور قیامت کے دن پر ایمان رکھتا ہو اور جو شخص اللہ سے ڈرتا ہے اللہ اس کے لیے چھٹکارے کی شکل نکال دیتا ہے

English Translation:

“And when they have [nearly] fulfilled their term, either retain them according to acceptable terms or part with them according to acceptable terms. And bring to witness two just men from among you and establish the testimony for [the acceptance of] Allah. That is instructed to whoever should believe in Allah and the Last day. And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that whoever fears Him—He will make for him a way out.

Taqwa in difficult passages of life is met with an exit crafted by Allah.

Even when situations feel sealed, God-consciousness opens paths.

Reflection

Fear Allah in your choices, and trust Him to make a way out.

Exits appear for hearts that honor Him.

Reference: Quran 65:2, Surah At-Talaaq.

Quran 65:3

Surah At-Talaaq (سُورَةُ الطَّلَاقِ)

Arabic:

وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدۡرࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

اور اسے ایسی جگہ سے روزی دیتا ہے جس کا اسے گمان بھی نہ ہو اور جو شخص اللہ پر توکل کرے گا اللہ اسے کافی ہوگا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ اپنا کام پورا کرکے ہی رہے گا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ نے ہر چیز کا ایک اندازه مقرر کر رکھا ہے

English Translation:

“And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us He will provide from where one does not expect—and whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient. Allah will accomplish His purpose, and He has set a measure for everything.

Tawakkul meets unexpected provision and the sufficiency of Allah Himself.

His purpose is never frustrated.

Reflection

Rely on Allah—He is enough, and provision may arrive from unseen directions.

His purpose will be fulfilled in its measured time.

Reference: Quran 65:3, Surah At-Talaaq.

Quran 94:5

Surah Ash-Sharh (سُورَةُ الشَّرۡحِ)

Arabic:

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا

Urdu Translation:

پس یقیناً مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے

English Translation:

“For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that indeed, with hardship comes ease.

Ease is paired with difficulty—not merely after it in some distant age, but accompanying it by His wisdom.

Hardship is never the whole of the story Allah writes.

Reflection

Look for the ease that travels with your hardship.

Allah has promised that difficulty does not stand alone.

Reference: Quran 94:5, Surah Ash-Sharh.

Quran 94:6

Surah Ash-Sharh (سُورَةُ الشَّرۡحِ)

Arabic:

إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

بیشک مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے

English Translation:

“Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us again: indeed, with hardship comes ease.

The repetition seals certainty—ease is sure, not speculative.

What presses you now is already accompanied by relief Allah has placed beside it.

Reflection

Hear the promise twice: with hardship is ease.

Hold that certainty when the difficulty feels endless.

Reference: Quran 94:6, Surah Ash-Sharh.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3524

Book 48: Chapters on Supplication (كتاب الدعوات عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Narrated Anas bin Malik said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

يَا حَىُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ

Urdu Translation:

اے زندہ اور ہمیشہ رہنے والے! تیری رحمت کے وسیلے سے تیری مدد چاہتا ہوں

English Translation:

“O Living, O Self-Sustaining Sustainer! In Your Mercy do I seek relief (Yā Ḥayyu yā Qayyūm, bi-raḥmatika astaghīth)”

Meaning ❤️

This is what the Prophet ﷺ himself said when something weighed heavily on him.

He called on Allah by two names: al-Hayy (the Ever-Living, who never tires) and al-Qayyum (who holds everything up), and asked for rescue through His mercy.

Reflection

When it feels like you're going under, say it: Ya Hayyu ya Qayyum, bi rahmatika astaghith.

The One who holds up the heavens can hold you up too.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3524, Book of Chapters on Supplication.
