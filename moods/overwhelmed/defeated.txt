Quran 3:139

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ

English Translation:

“So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are [true] believers”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us not to weaken and not to grieve—for you will be superior if you are true believers.

Low spirits after setbacks are met with a call to rise again in faith.

Belief itself becomes a source of dignity and strength.

Reflection

Do not let grief convince you that you are finished.

True faith lifts the heart above despair.

Reference: Quran 3:139, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 3:146

Surah Aal-i-Imraan (سُورَةُ آلِ عِمۡرَانَ)

Arabic:

وَكَأَيِّن مِّن نَّبِيࣲّ قَٰتَلَ مَعَهُۥ رِبِّيُّونَ كَثِيرࣱ فَمَا وَهَنُواْ لِمَآ أَصَابَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا ضَعُفُواْ وَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ

English Translation:

“And how many a prophet [fought and] with him fought many religious scholars. But they never lost assurance due to what afflicted them in the cause of Allah, nor did they weaken or submit. And Allah loves the steadfast”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us of prophets and devoted companions who never lost assurance due to what afflicted them in Allah’s way; they did not weaken or humiliate themselves—and Allah loves the patient.

Steadfastness under wound and loss is beloved to Allah.

Faith can remain upright when circumstances bow the body.

Reflection

Do not let affliction steal your assurance in Allah.

Patience under pain is something He loves.

Reference: Quran 3:146, Surah Aal-i-Imraan.

Quran 47:7

Surah Muhammad (سُورَةُ مُحَمَّدٍ)

Arabic:

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ

English Translation:

“O you who have believed, if you support Allah, He will support you and plant firmly your feet”

Meaning ❤️

Allah tells us that the believers that if they support Allah, He will support them and plant firmly their feet.

Supporting Allah means upholding His religion, truth, and commands. In return comes His aid and stability.

Firm footing is a gift that follows sincere support of His cause.

Reflection

Stand for what Allah loves, and He will steady your steps.

His support meets those who support His way.

Reference: Quran 47:7, Surah Muhammad.
