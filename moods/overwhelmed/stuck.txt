Quran 94:5-8

Surah Ash-Sharh (سُورَةُ الشَّرۡحِ)

Arabic:

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا ﴿٥﴾ إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرࣰ ا ﴿٦﴾ فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ ﴿٧﴾ وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب ﴿٨﴾

Urdu Translation:

(5) پس یقیناً مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے (6) بیشک مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے (7) پس جب تو فارغ ہو تو عبادت میں محنت کر (8) اور اپنے پروردگار ہی کی طرف دل لگا

English Translation:

“(5) For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease (6) Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease (7) So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship] (8) And to your Lord direct [your] longing”

Meaning ❤️

Allah promises twice: with hardship comes ease. Then He says: when you are done, stand up again, and turn your longing to your Lord.

Scholars note 'hardship' is definite and 'ease' indefinite — one hardship cannot overcome two eases.

Reflection

Ease is coming with this hardship, not after it.

Take the next step, and turn your hope to Him.

Reference: Quran 94:5-8, Surah Ash-Sharh.

Quran 18:10

Surah Al-Kahf (سُورَةُ الكَهۡفِ)

Arabic:

إِذۡ أَوَى ٱلۡفِتۡيَةُ إِلَى ٱلۡكَهۡفِ فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةࣰ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدࣰ ا

Urdu Translation:

ان چند نوجوانوں نے جب غار میں پناه لی تو دعا کی کہ اے ہمارے پروردگار! ہمیں اپنے پاس سے رحمت عطا فرما اور ہمارے کام میں ہمارے لئے راه یابی کو آسان کردے

English Translation:

“[Mention] when the youths retreated to the cave and said, "Our Lord, grant us from Yourself mercy and prepare for us from our affair right guidance”

Meaning ❤️

The young believers of the cave, with nowhere left to go, made one du'a: Our Lord, give us mercy from Yourself and set right our affair for us.

Allah answered with a way out no one could have planned.

Reflection

When you can't see the way forward, make their du'a.

Rabbana atina min ladunka rahmah, wa hayyi' lana min amrina rashada.

Reference: Quran 18:10, Surah Al-Kahf.

Quran 29:69

Surah Al-Ankaboot (سُورَةُ العَنكَبُوتِ)

Arabic:

وَٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ فِينَا لَنَهۡدِيَنَّهُمۡ سُبُلَنَاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ

Urdu Translation:

اور جو لوگ ہماری راه میں مشقتیں برداشت کرتے ہیں ہم انہیں اپنی راہیں ضرور دکھا دیں گے۔ یقیناً اللہ تعالیٰ نیکو کاروں کا ساتھی ہے

English Translation:

“And those who strive for Us - We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good”

Meaning ❤️

Allah promises those who strive for Him: We will surely guide them to Our paths, and Allah is with those who do good.

The path opens to the one who keeps moving toward Him.

Reflection

You don't need the whole map — just keep striving for Him.

He has promised to show you the way.

Reference: Quran 29:69, Surah Al-Ankaboot.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3505

Book 48: Chapters on Supplication (كتاب الدعوات عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)

Ibrahim bin Muhammad bin Sa`d narrated from his father, from Sa`d that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said (رضي الله عنه/عنهم):

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

Arabic:

دَعْوَةُ ذِي النُّونِ إِذْ دَعَا وَهُوَ فِي بَطْنِ الْحُوتِ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ . فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَدْعُ بِهَا رَجُلٌ مُسْلِمٌ فِي شَيْءٍ قَطُّ إِلاَّ اسْتَجَابَ اللَّهُ لَهُ

Urdu Translation:

ذوالنون ( یونس علیہ السلام ) کی دعا جو انہوں نے مچھلی کے پیٹ میں رہنے کے دوران کی تھی وہ یہ تھی: «لا إله إلا أنت سبحانك إني كنت من الظالمين» ”تیرے سوا کوئی معبود برحق نہیں، تو پاک ہے، میں ہی ظالم ( خطاکار ) ہوں“، کیونکہ یہ ایسی دعا ہے کہ جب بھی کوئی مسلمان شخص اسے پڑھ کر دعا کرے گا تو اللہ تعالیٰ اس کی دعا قبول فرمائے گا

English Translation:

“The supplication of Dhun-Nun (Prophet Yunus) when he supplicated, while in the belly of the whale was: ‘There is none worthy of worship except You, Glory to You, Indeed, I have been of the transgressors. (Lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu minaẓ-ẓālimīn)’ So indeed, no Muslim man supplicates with it for anything, ever, except Allah responds to him”

Meaning ❤️

No one was ever more stuck than Yunus (as) — in darkness, inside a whale, at the bottom of the sea. Allah brought him out.

The Prophet ﷺ promises that this same du'a is answered for anyone who makes it.

Reflection

Whatever you are stuck in, it is not deeper than the belly of the whale.

La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3505, Book of Chapters on Supplication.
